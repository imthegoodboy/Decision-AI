4.1 Attribution was already at ceiling

In every condition about 98% of cited claims were fully supported by the cited source and under 1% of factual statements were unsupported (Table 1; Appendix Figure B1); no difference approached significance. Even for the baseline, {{pct:baseline_bindable_share}} of factual claims could be bound to a supporting passage. With full source text, a current frontier model no longer shows the citation failures earlier audits found [1, 2]; a verification layer must earn its keep elsewhere.

4.2 Planted falsehoods: flagged by everyone, repeated only without the tool

Every poisoned-pool answer cited the planted article, almost always to dispute it, and every one disclosed conflicting evidence. Still, {{pct0:poisoned.planted_adopted_any.baseline}} of baseline and {{pct0:poisoned.planted_adopted_any.selfcheck}} of self-check answers stated at least one planted falsehood as fact, against {{pct0:poisoned.planted_adopted_any.decisionai}} of tool answers (Figure 3). The repeated falsehoods were used as reasons: an invented $9.99 renewal price made a password manager "the priciest mainstream option", and a false claim that an e-bike "labours on sustained grades" was grounds to rule it out. With {{raw:n_questions}} questions this difference is not significant (exact McNemar {{p:mcnemar.planted_adopted_any_vs_baseline}}). Every tool run recorded explicit conflicts with the planted page (mean {{raw1:tool_process.planted_conflicts_mean}} per run), which the fixed rules turned into non-citable contradicted claims. On clean pools, where only genuine disagreements exist, the self-check prompt raised conflict disclosure from {{pct0:metrics.conflict_disclosed.clean.baseline}} to {{pct0:metrics.conflict_disclosed.clean.selfcheck}}; the tool did not raise it further.

4.4 Which reliability signals can be gamed?

The planted articles carried exactly the metadata that reliability heuristics reward: a named author, a publisher and a recent date. On metadata alone they scored {{raw1:reliability_probe.planted_prior_mean}} on average, against {{raw1:reliability_probe.genuine_prior_mean}} for genuine sources, and ranked above the median genuine source in {{rawpct:reliability_probe.planted_above_genuine_median_prior}} of questions (Figure 5). Many genuine pages, including independent lab reviews, carried no author or date. Verification moved planted pages by {{raw1:reliability_probe.planted_earned_delta_mean}} points on average and genuine sources by {{raw1:reliability_probe.genuine_earned_delta_mean}} (one-sided Mann–Whitney {{pv:reliability_probe.mannwhitney_p}}), and the planted page then ranked above the genuine median in {{rawpct:reliability_probe.planted_above_genuine_median_final}} of questions. Earned reliability halved the attacker's advantage but did not remove it. The ledgers show why: a source loses reliability only for claims the agent checks against it, and when a genuine claim conflicts with a planted one and has no second corroborating source, the fixed rule marks the genuine claim as contradicted.

4.5 Process and validity checks

All {{raw:tool_process.runs}} tool runs completed the required process and sealed an intact hash chain. Agents verified {{int:tool_process.claims_min}}–{{int:tool_process.claims_max}} claims per run ({{int:tool_process.claims_total}} in total). Every recorded quote was located verbatim, and the rules marked {{rawpct:tool_process.contradicted_share}} of recorded claims as contradicted. The judge reported {{raw:judging.false_positive_planted}} planted-claim assertions in the {{raw:judging.false_positive_checks}} checks on clean-pool answers, which never saw the planted article.
