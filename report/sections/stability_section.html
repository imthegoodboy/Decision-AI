4.3 Flagged but still followed: planted sources steer the recommendation

Agents disputed the planted claims, but their final choices still moved (Figure 6). Between two independent runs on the same clean sources, the recommended option changed in {{rawpct:stability.systems.baseline.flip_replicate}} of questions in every condition. Adding the planted article changed it in {{rawpct:stability.systems.baseline.flip_poisoned}} (baseline), {{rawpct:stability.systems.selfcheck.flip_poisoned}} (self-check) and {{rawpct:stability.systems.decisionai.flip_poisoned}} (tool) of questions. Across all question–condition pairs, {{rawpct:stability.pooled.attack_consistent_poisoned}} of poisoned runs moved in the direction the planted article pushed (away from an option it falsely disparaged or toward one it falsely promoted), against {{rawpct:stability.pooled.attack_consistent_replicate}} of clean replicates. At the question level, {{raw:stability.question_level.questions_more_attack_shifts_poisoned}} of {{raw:n_questions}} questions showed more attacker-direction shifts under poisoning and none showed fewer (sign test {{pv:stability.question_level.sign_test_p}}).

In the savings-account question the planted article falsely claimed that Marcus offers no same-day transfers and charges for withdrawals. All three conditions switched from Marcus to a competitor while labelling those claims unverified or contradicted. The password-manager question showed the same pattern after false claims about Bitwarden. The tool reduced steering but did not remove it: {{rawpct:stability.systems.decisionai.attack_consistent_poisoned}} attacker-direction shifts against {{rawpct:stability.systems.decisionai.attack_consistent_replicate}} for its clean replicate, compared with {{rawpct:stability.systems.baseline.attack_consistent_poisoned}} against {{rawpct:stability.systems.baseline.attack_consistent_replicate}} for the baseline. The tool's ranking shows where the influence entered. Tool-using agents recommended the evidence-weighted leader in {{rawpct:ranking_agreement.clean_rate}} of clean runs but only {{rawpct:ranking_agreement.poisoned_rate}} of poisoned runs, typically overriding it in favour of an option the planted article had not attacked. A planted source does not need to be believed: doubt alone tips a close decision. Defences scored only by whether a falsehood is repeated [14, 15] would rate these agents robust while their decisions were steered.
