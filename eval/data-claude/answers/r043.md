I recommend the **Honeywell Air Touch V5**, which Digit lists at about ₹10,199 on Amazon [1].

**Why the V5**
- **CADR:** It is rated at 380 m³/h and is described as suited to large rooms [1]. Digit's rule of thumb is a CADR of at least two-thirds of the room's square footage, which is about 233 m³/h for 350 sq ft [1]. Smartprix says to aim for 3–4 air changes per hour in Delhi [2]. By my own arithmetic, a 350 sq ft room with an 8-foot ceiling holds about 79 m³, so 380 m³/h gives roughly 4–5 changes per hour.
- **Filtration:** It has an H13 HEPA filter plus a pre-filter and carbon [1]. Smartprix calls True HEPA H13 non-negotiable and warns against "HEPA-type" labels. It also says you need real activated carbon for Delhi's gases and VOCs [2].
- **Bedroom suitability:** It is listed at about 22 dB, well under the 30 dB Digit calls ideal for bedrooms [1].
- **Budget:** It leaves about ₹4,800 of headroom under ₹15,000 [1].

**Caveat on filter cost**
The sources give no price for the V5's own replacement filters, so I can't confirm that criterion. Digit says filters generally cost ₹1,500–5,000 per year and advises checking local availability before buying [1]. Smartprix lists Honeywell's larger U2 filter set at about ₹8,400 [2], so check the V5's filter price and stock before you order. Also, Digit's picks are based on advertised specs, not hands-on testing [1].

**Alternatives**
- **Qubo Q600 (₹14,490):** CADR is 450 m³/h with H13 HEPA, carbon and UV, but it runs at about 55 dB [1]. Qubo's bigger Q1000 is praised for a 15,000-hour filter life, which means low running costs [2]. The sources don't give the Q600's filter life.
- **Eureka Forbes 355 (₹9,999):** CADR is 355 m³/h with H13 HEPA, but noise is high at 63 dB [1]. It is the cheapest strong-CADR option.
- **Mi Air Purifier 4 (₹14,999 on Flipkart, ₹17,999 on Amazon):** CADR is 387 m³/h, noise is about 32 dB, and it has app control [1]. Only the Flipkart price fits your budget.
- **Coway Airmega AIM (₹12,499):** CADR is 303 m³/h with H13 HEPA, and it is quiet and reliable [1]. A Flipkart listing claims an 8,500-hour filter life, roughly 1.5–2 years [5]. It is the lowest-CADR option here, though still adequate for your room by Digit's rule of thumb.
- **Lower-CADR models to avoid:** The Qubo Q200, at 150 m³/h and rated for up to 200 sq ft, is too small for 350 sq ft [5]. Realme's TechLife uses only H12 HEPA, so it fails your True HEPA requirement [6].

**Tips**
- Keep the purifier at least 1 ft from walls and keep windows closed [1].
- On smog days, run it manually on a medium or high setting rather than relying on auto mode, then drop to a quieter mode [2].
- Prices fluctuate, so confirm the final price before buying [1].
