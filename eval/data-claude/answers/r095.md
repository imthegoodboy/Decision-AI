I recommend the **Qubo Q600**, with the **Honeywell Air Touch V5** as the cheaper fallback. This is based on advertised specs only, because Digit says it did no hands-on testing [1].

**Why the Qubo Q600.** It has the highest CADR among the models Digit lists under ₹15,000: 450 m³/h. It has a HEPA H13 filter plus pre-filter, carbon and UV stages, and covers a listed 600 sq ft. It costs ₹14,490 on Amazon, just under your cap [1]. Digit's rule of thumb is a CADR of at least two-thirds of the room area in sq ft, which is about 233 m³/h for 350 sq ft [1]. The Q600 clears that by a wide margin.

**Where the sources disagree.** Smartprix is much stricter. It wants 3–4 air changes per hour for Delhi and says 400 m³/h suits a 250 sq ft bedroom [2]. Scaled up to 350 sq ft, that would imply well over 450 m³/h. That scaling is my own extrapolation, not a figure from the source. Smartprix also warns that brands' advertised coverage figures are often marketing numbers [2]. Digit's coverage figures are exactly those advertised numbers [1]. I would trust the CADR figures over the coverage claims. For Delhi, Smartprix's conservative view is the safer guide, so the Q600's headroom is a real advantage. Neither source gives a measured result for a 350 sq ft room, so this is a judgement call.

**Replacement filters: the weak spot.** I found no Q600 filter price or lifespan in the sources, so I can't confirm it meets your "affordable filters" requirement. Other Qubo models have long filter lives. The Q200 is listed at 9,000 hours [5], and Smartprix lists the Q1000 at 15,000 hours [2]. That suggests Qubo filters may be cheap to run, but it is not proof for the Q600. Digit gives only a general figure of ₹1,500–5,000 per year for replacement filters, and advises checking local availability before buying [1]. Check the Q600's filter price and stock before you order.

**Bedroom noise.** Digit lists the Q600 at about 55 dB [1]. It doesn't say whether that is the maximum speed, and it considers under 30 dB ideal for bedrooms [1]. Plan to run it on a lower setting at night.

**Alternatives**
- **Honeywell Air Touch V5.** It has a CADR of 380 m³/h, H13 HEPA and about 22 dB, at ₹10,199 [1]. It is quieter and cheaper, and still above Digit's minimum. Its filter cost isn't given in these sources, and the 22 dB figure is manufacturer-advertised and unverified.
- **Eureka Forbes 355.** It has a CADR of 355 m³/h, H13 and a ₹9,999 price, but Digit notes the noise is high at 63 dB [1].
- **Mi Air Purifier 4.** It has a CADR of 387 m³/h and is listed at ₹14,999 on Flipkart but ₹17,999 on Amazon [1]. Only the Flipkart price is under budget.
- **Cuckoo Respure.** It has a CADR of 408 m³/h at ₹9,889, but Digit lists only "HEPA", not H13 [1]. Both Digit and Smartprix warn against vague HEPA labels [1][2].

Prices change often, so confirm them at checkout [1].
