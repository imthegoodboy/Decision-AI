## Recommendation
Buy the **Redmi 15 5G** (from Rs 15,999) if battery life and price lead your list [1]. If long software support matters more than battery, choose the **Samsung Galaxy M17 5G**. The evidence-weighted ranking put the M17 narrowly first (68 vs 66 out of 100). I still lead with the Redmi because none of the sources gives the M17's price, so I can't confirm it fits under Rs 20,000. The sources also contain no verified camera claim for the M17.

## Why
- The Redmi 15 5G has a 7,000mAh battery, which Croma calls one of the biggest in the segment [1].
- It starts at Rs 15,999, leaving room under budget for the 256GB version Croma suggests for photos [1].
- It has 50MP dual rear cameras [1]. That is a spec only. No source tests indoor or low-light shots.
- Samsung says the Galaxy M17 5G gets 6 OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates [4]. That is far longer than the Redmi's promise.

## Trade-offs
- The Redmi promises only 2 years of OS updates and 4 years of security updates [1]. That is the weakest part of the case given your priorities.
- The M17 has a 5000mAh battery with 25W charging [4], which is smaller than the Redmi's 7,000mAh [1].
- I found no independent indoor camera testing for any phone, so camera confidence is low.

## Alternatives
- **Galaxy M17 5G:** best for update length [4]. Check the price first.
- **Galaxy A17 5G:** it has a 50MP main camera with OIS and a 5000mAh battery [4]. Croma calls its software promise one of the best in the segment but gives no number of years [1]. The camera detail comes only from Samsung's own page [4].
- **Oppo K13 5G:** it has a 7,000mAh battery, confirmed by two sources [1][2]. Its price is disputed (see below), and I found no update or camera claims for it.
- **Vivo T4x 5G:** a 6,500mAh battery, confirmed by two sources [1][2].
- **Avoid for your needs:** The Realme P4x has a "rather weak" camera setup [1]. The Lava Blaze Duo 3 has limited OS support [5].
- **Samsung Galaxy A07 5G:** Beebom lists Android 16 with 6 years of promised updates, a decent main camera and a battery that lasts over a day. It also lists a 720p LCD and soft selfies, and calls it a hard recommendation for the price [5]. This is a single-source claim.

## Conflicting evidence
- **Oppo K13 price:** Croma says it starts at Rs 19,999 [1]. Digit lists Rs 24,999 for the 8GB/256GB variant [2]. The tool marked the Croma figure as contradicted. Digit is undated and Croma is dated February 2026, so I can't tell which is current. It may be a variant difference. Treat the K13 as possibly over budget.
- **Source bias:** The Samsung update claims come from Samsung itself [4]. Croma is a retailer [1]. Both are less neutral than independent reviews, and the independent sources here (Digit [2], Beebom [5]) are undated.

## Before you decide
- Check the current prices of the M17 and A17, since the sources don't give them.
- Confirm Samsung's update policy on the official spec page.
- Look at independent low-light camera samples. Indoor shots of kids are the weakest-evidenced part of this recommendation.
