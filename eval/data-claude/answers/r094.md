**Recommendation: Honeywell Air Touch V5 (about ₹10,199 on Amazon)**

It is the best fit for a 350 sq ft Delhi bedroom within your budget. It has a CADR of 380 m³/h, a HEPA H13 filter with pre-filter and carbon, and a rated noise level of about 22 dB. That is quiet enough for sleeping, and Digit calls it suited to large rooms [1].

**Why it fits your priorities**
- **CADR:** Digit's rule of thumb is a CADR of at least two-thirds of the room's area in sq ft. For 350 sq ft that is roughly 235 m³/h [1]. The V5 clears that comfortably. Smartprix advises 3–4 air changes per hour for Delhi and says to oversize rather than undersize [2]. By my own estimate, the V5 delivers close to 5 changes per hour in a room this size.
- **True HEPA:** Both guides say to insist on true HEPA H13 or H14 and avoid "HEPA-type" labels [1][2]. Smartprix adds that you need a substantial carbon filter for Delhi's VOCs and fumes [2]. The V5 lists both [1].
- **Bedroom noise:** Digit treats anything below 30 dB as ideal for bedrooms [1]. The V5 is well under that, while several high-CADR rivals are not.

**Caveat on filter costs:** The sources give no replacement-filter price for the V5. Digit says filters generally cost ₹1,500–5,000 a year and advises checking local availability before buying [1]. Honeywell's larger U2 needs a filter set costing over ₹8,000 [2], so confirm the V5's filter price and stock before you order. Digit's picks are also based on advertised specs, not hands-on testing [1].

**Alternatives**
- **Qubo Q600 (₹14,490):** It has the highest CADR in budget at 450 m³/h, plus H13 [1]. It is louder at about 55 dB and uses most of your budget. Choose it if speed matters more than quiet.
- **Eureka Forbes 355 (₹9,999):** It has 355 m³/h, H13 and the lowest price here. Digit says the noise levels are high, at 63 dB [1]. This is a poor fit for sleeping.
- **Coway Airmega AIM (₹12,499):** It has a 303 m³/h CADR and H13 [1]. A Flipkart listing claims a long 8,500-hour filter life [5], which helps running costs. Its CADR is lower than the V5's.
- **Qubo Q1000:** It has 600 m³/h, H13 and a 15,000-hour filter life, which gives very low running costs [2]. I could not verify its price in these sources, so check that it is under ₹15,000.
- **Mi Air Purifier 4 (₹14,999 on Flipkart):** It has 387 m³/h and is quiet at about 32 dB. It is ₹17,999 on Amazon [1]. The sources do not confirm its filter grade.

Avoid very small models for this room, such as the Qubo Q200 at 150 m³/h and 200 sq ft coverage [5].

**Placement:** Keep the unit at least 1 ft from walls and close the windows while it runs [1]. On smoggy days, run it on a higher manual setting for an hour before dropping to a quieter mode [2].
