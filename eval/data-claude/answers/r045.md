## Recommendation
Buy the **Eureka Forbes 355** (Rs 9,999 on Amazon). If bedroom noise matters more to you than an explicit "True HEPA" label, the **Honeywell Air Touch V5** (Rs 10,199) is the closest alternative. Both are well under Rs 15,000. Confidence is moderate, because every model-specific spec here comes from one article that did not test the products.

## Why
- The Eureka Forbes 355 has a CADR of 355 m³/h [1]. Digit's rule of thumb is a CADR of at least two-thirds of the room area in sq ft, assuming an 8-foot ceiling [1]. For 350 sq ft that works out to about 233 m³/h (my calculation), so this model clears it comfortably.
- It is the only budget pick that Digit explicitly describes as having a True HEPA H13 filter [1]. Both sources say to insist on True HEPA H13/H14 and to avoid "HEPA-type" filters [1][2].
- It costs Rs 9,999 on Amazon [1]. That leaves room in the budget for spare filters.
- The Honeywell Air Touch V5 has a CADR of 380 m³/h and a rated noise level of about 22 dB [1], and costs Rs 10,199 [1]. Digit lists it with a "HEPA H13" filter but does not use the words "True HEPA" for it [1].

## Trade-offs
- The Eureka Forbes is loud at 63 dB, and Digit flags the noise [1]. Digit says under 30 dB is generally ideal for bedrooms [1]. On this priority the Honeywell wins.
- The sources give no replacement-filter price for either model. Digit only gives a general range of Rs 1,500 to Rs 5,000 per year [1]. This was one of your three priorities, so it is a real gap.
- Digit says its picks are based purely on advertised specifications, not hands-on testing [1]. The 22 dB and CADR figures are therefore unverified.

## Alternatives
- **Honeywell Air Touch V5:** the quietest option, with higher CADR on paper [1].
- **Qubo Q600:** CADR 450 m³/h with HEPA H13, at Rs 14,490 [1]. That is near your ceiling, and Digit lists it at ~55 dB [1]. It is the strongest on CADR but not the best value.
- **Mi Air Purifier 4:** Rs 14,999 on Flipkart but Rs 17,999 on Amazon [1], so it can exceed your budget depending on where you buy.
- **Qubo Q1000:** Smartprix praises its 15,000-hour filter life and says it has True HEPA H13 [2]. No price was given in the retrieved text, so I could not confirm it fits your budget.

## Conflicting evidence
- **CADR sizing:** Digit's two-thirds rule [1] is much more lenient than Smartprix's advice. Smartprix says a 400 m³/h CADR suits a 250 sq ft bedroom and suggests oversizing for Delhi [2]. [4] is a duplicate copy of [2], so it adds no independent support. Scaled to 350 sq ft, Smartprix's standard would favour the Qubo Q600 (450 m³/h) over the 355 and 380 m³/h models. I rate Digit as more reliable than Smartprix, but Smartprix's guidance is tuned to Delhi's heavier pollution. Treat the 355 and 380 m³/h models as adequate rather than generous.
- **Coway Airmega AIM:** Digit lists it at CADR 303 m³/h [1]. A Flipkart listing says 230 cmph [5]. I could not resolve this, and the Flipkart page is the less reliable source, so I did not recommend it.
