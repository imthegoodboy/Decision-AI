**Recommendation: take PTE Academic UKVI if your university accepts it, and IELTS for UKVI if it doesn't or if you want the safer choice. Your two goals point in different directions, and the sources can't settle this for India specifically.**

**Both are valid for a student visa.** For most UK visas the Home Office accepts only a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider, taken at an approved centre [1][7]. A normal IELTS won't do. You must book the UKVI version, though the content is the same as the standard test [7]. Both IELTS for UKVI and PTE Academic UKVI are approved for student visas [1][6][7].

**Speed favours PTE.** One guide says PTE results often arrive within 48 hours [1]. Pearson says 85.7% of test takers got results within 2 days, but that figure is from 2018 and comes from the test provider itself [3]. Pearson also says some results take longer because of extra review or security checks [3]. None of the sources gives IELTS turnaround times, so I can't quantify the speed advantage. The sources also give nothing specific to India, such as seat availability or waiting times for a test date.

**Acceptance is where the sources disagree.**
- IDP, which co-owns IELTS, says IELTS is accepted by over 13,500 organisations against about 3,000 for PTE. It concludes IELTS is the choice for widest acceptance [6].
- Other sources treat the two as equally valid for UK visas [1][7]. Pearson claims PTE Academic UKVI is accepted for all UK visas [3].

I would give IDP's figures limited weight. IDP sells IELTS, and the figures are global counts of organisations, not UK visa acceptance. I'd say IELTS is probably the more widely recognised test overall, but for a UK student visa the evidence shows the two are equally valid.

**What decides it is your university.** For degree-level study you need CEFR B2. Universities that are Higher Education Providers may assess your English themselves, which is why some accept non-SELT tests such as TOEFL iBT, Duolingo or Cambridge C1 Advanced [7]. Sheffield, for example, accepts all IELTS qualifications for direct entry [2]. Always check your offer letter first [7].

**Other options.** Other approved SELTs are LanguageCert, Trinity (in the UK only) and PSI Skills for English (outside the UK) [7]. An older Sheffield page lists only IELTS, Trinity, PTE and LanguageCert, probably because it predates later changes [2]. I'd trust the newer list [7], which was checked in September 2026. I'd still confirm the current list on GOV.UK.

**Practical steps:**
1. Confirm which tests your university and course accept.
2. Choose the UKVI version and an approved centre [1][7].
3. Check the score your route requires on GOV.UK [7].
4. The result must be from the two years before you apply [7].
5. Leave time for a retake.
