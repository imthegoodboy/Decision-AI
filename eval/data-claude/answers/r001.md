I recommend the **Samsung Galaxy M17 5G**, or the closely related **Galaxy A17 5G** if you prefer it. Neither source gives their prices, so check that they fit under ₹20,000 before you buy.

**Why this pick**

- **Software updates:** This is your most reliable differentiator. Samsung lists 6 generations of OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates for the M17 [4]. Croma calls the A17 5G's update promise one of the best in the segment [1]. The Redmi 15 5G promises only 2 OS updates and 4 years of security updates [1].
- **Indoor photos of your kids:** Both Samsung phones use a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilisation (OIS), plus a 13MP front camera [4]. OIS generally helps in dim rooms, but that is my inference. Neither source tests indoor or low-light shots. Croma also says the A17 stands out for its cameras and AI features [1]. Samsung's own page is marketing, so treat its camera claims with some caution.
- **Battery:** Both have a 5000mAh battery [4]. That is a solid all-day battery but not the largest in the segment. Samsung says the M17 charges at 25W [4].
- **Extras:** The M17 has a bright Super AMOLED display, Gorilla Glass Victus and IP54 protection [4].

**Alternatives**

- **Redmi 15 5G (from ₹15,999):** Choose this if battery matters more than updates. It has a 7,000mAh battery, a 144Hz display and an IP64 rating [1]. Croma suggests the 256GB version to hold 50MP photos [1].
- **Oppo K13 5G:** It also has a 7,000mAh battery, 80W charging, a 120Hz OLED screen and a 16MP front camera [1]. It starts at ₹19,999 [1], though Digit lists a higher price of ₹24,999 [2]. Check the current price.
- **Vivo T4x 5G (from ₹15,499):** It has a 6,500mAh battery and a Dimensity 7300 chip [1]. Digit's pros and cons list "long lasting battery life", "decent cameras" and a "cluttered UI" for a phone matching its 6,500mAh spec [2].
- **Galaxy A07 5G:** It has a 6-year update promise and over a day of battery [5]. However, it has a 720p LCD screen and soft selfies, which Beebom says make it a hard recommendation at its price [5].

**Phones to skip**

- **Realme P4x 5G:** Croma calls its camera setup "rather weak" [1].
- **Vivo T5 Lite 5G:** Beebom reports slow, laggy performance and heavy bloatware [5].

**Caveats**

The sources don't include hands-on battery tests or low-light camera comparisons. Before buying, look for an independent review of the M17 or A17's indoor shots and real-world battery life. Also confirm the current price and the RAM and storage version.
