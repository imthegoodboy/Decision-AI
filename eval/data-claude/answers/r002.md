**Recommendation: Samsung Galaxy A17 5G, with the Galaxy M17 5G as a close sibling.**

**Why the A17.** You ranked software updates and photos highly, and Samsung is strongest there.
- Croma calls the A17's software update promise "one of the best" in this segment. It also credits the phone's cameras and AI features [1]. Croma doesn't give the number of years, so check the exact policy before you buy.
- Samsung lists a 50MP wide camera with OIS, a 13MP front camera and a 5000mAh battery [4]. OIS should help with steadier shots in dim rooms. No source tests indoor or low-light photos of children, so that is my inference.
- Samsung's own page is marketing, so I weighed its claims less than Croma's independent view [1][4].

**Battery trade-off.** Battery is the A17's weak point. Samsung says most phones in this segment have a 5000mAh battery [4]. Croma and Bajaj Finance show rivals with 6,500–7,000mAh [1][3]. The A17 matches Samsung's "typical" figure but is smaller than the best of its rivals, and I found no battery-life test of it in these sources.

**Alternatives**
- **Galaxy M17 5G:** The same Samsung page lists a 50MP OIS camera and a 5000mAh battery with 25W charging. It also claims 6 generations of OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates [4]. That is the clearest long-term update promise in the sources, but it comes from the manufacturer alone. It is worth confirming the price, since the sources don't give one.
- **Redmi 15 5G:** If battery matters most, it has a 7,000mAh battery and starts at Rs 15,999. Updates are only 2 years of OS and 4 years of security. It has 50MP dual rear cameras and an 8MP front camera [1]. It is the weaker choice for updates and probably for selfies.
- **Oppo K13 5G:** It also has a 7,000mAh battery, but the sources disagree on price. Croma says it starts at Rs 19,999 [1], while Digit lists ₹24,999 [2]. Check the current price, as it may be over budget.
- **Samsung Galaxy A07 5G:** Beebom notes Android 16 with 6 years of promised updates and battery that lasts more than a day. It also flags a 720p LCD and soft selfies, and calls it a hard recommendation at its price [5].
- **Avoid:** Realme P4x, whose camera setup Croma calls weak [1].

**Where sources disagree or are thin**
- Digit's table shows weak camera scores for some phones [2], and Beebom's picks don't focus on indoor photography [5]. The camera-phone lists don't settle which phone is best for your use.
- Only the Samsung page gives explicit update years, for the M17. Treat that as a claim to verify, not an independent fact.
- Prices change quickly. Confirm current Rs 20,000 pricing and bank offers before buying.
