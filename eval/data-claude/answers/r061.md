## Recommendation: Bitwarden Families

For a family of four on iPhone, Android and Windows, I recommend **Bitwarden Families**. It scores well on the three things you named: track record, independent audits and price.

**Audits and transparency.** Bitwarden's whole codebase (clients, server, extensions) is open source and has been audited multiple times by independent firms [5]. Another review says it publishes Cure53 audit results and holds SOC 2 Type 2 [6][8]. One source claims a January 2026 Cure53 audit with zero critical findings [2]. That source is less rigorous than the others, so check Bitwarden's own audit page before you rely on that date.

**Price.** Bitwarden is among the cheapest reputable options, but the sources disagree on the exact figure. One lists the Families plan at $40 a year for six users [2][5]. A September 2026 check of vendor pricing pages lists $47.88 a year [8]. Either way it covers your four people with room for two more, and it costs far less than 1Password Families at $5.99 a month for five users [8]. The free tier also has unlimited devices and passwords [2].

**Platforms.** There are native iOS and Android apps with biometric unlock and system autofill, plus Windows, macOS and Linux desktop apps [2]. The main drawbacks are a less polished interface than 1Password [5] and email-only support for free users [2].

**Alternatives**

- **1Password Families** is the pick if you will pay more for polish. It is described as the most polished, audited option, with a Secret Key design that keeps a server breach from exposing your vault. It publishes Cure53 audits and holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 [8]. The cost is about $5.99 a month for five people, and the $4.49 first-year price rises at renewal [8].
- **RoboForm Family** has the lowest list price, about $1.59 a month for five users. It reports no breaches in 25 years and has passed a third-party audit [1][4]. Its interface is dated, and some sharing features only work with other RoboForm users [4].
- **NordPass Family** covers six users and uses XChaCha20 encryption, with a published third-party audit [1][8]. Its promotional prices rise at renewal [1], so compare renewal rates rather than the first-year price [8].
- **Proton Pass** is open source and third-party audited, but it costs more for a family [2][8].

**Avoid LastPass.** Its 2022 breach exposed encrypted vaults plus unencrypted URLs and metadata [6]. The UK ICO fined it £1.2 million [8], and a further third-party incident exposed customer contact data in June 2026, though vaults were reportedly unaffected [3]. The Keeper post that reports the 2026 incident is a competitor's marketing [3].

**Practical tips.** Use a long, unique master password for each family member, since vault strength depends on it [6]. Set up emergency access, which Bitwarden offers [2]. Confirm current prices on the vendor's site before you buy, because promotions and renewal rates change often [8].
