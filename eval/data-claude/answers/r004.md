I recommend the **Redmi 15 5G**. It starts at ₹15,999 and has a 7,000mAh battery, a 6.9-inch 144Hz display, a Snapdragon 6s Gen 3 chip and 50MP dual rear cameras [1]. One reviewer picked it as the most complete phone for battery, updates and family photos [5]. Buy the 256GB version, because a family photo library fills 128GB quickly [1][5].

**Battery:** This is its strongest point. A 7,000mAh phone is roughly a two-day device for most people, which helps when you are filming a school function or birthday [5]. Samsung's Galaxy A17 5G and M17 5G use 5,000mAh cells [4][5].

**Updates:** The sources disagree on the exact promise, so check it before you buy.
- One source says 2 years of OS updates and 4 years of security updates [1].
- Another says 4 years of OS updates and 6 years of security updates, which would put it level with Samsung's best [5].

**Indoor photos of the kids:** This is the weakest area, and none of the cheap options is clearly great. Dim rooms and wriggling children are hard for budget cameras, and megapixels matter less than image processing and stabilisation [5]. The Redmi's cameras are a 50MP main sensor plus a secondary lens, and its sources do not mention optical stabilisation [1][5]. I'd treat it as decent rather than outstanding, and look at low-light sample photos before buying.

**Alternatives**
- **Samsung Galaxy A17 5G:** It has one of the best update promises in the segment and a Super AMOLED screen [1][4]. The sources conflict on its camera. Samsung says the 50MP main camera has OIS [4]. The Rupee Gadget Desk review says it lacks OIS and struggles in dim rooms [5]. Its 5,000mAh battery is also smaller [4][5].
- **Samsung Galaxy M17 5G:** It has a 50MP OIS camera [4]. The update claims conflict. Samsung says 6 OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates [4], while the review says only 2 OS upgrades and 4 years of security patches [5]. It charges at just 25W [4][5].
- **Oppo K13 5G:** It has a 7,000mAh battery, 80W charging and a 120Hz OLED display, and a 16MP front camera [1][5]. It starts at ₹19,999, and higher-storage versions can cost more than ₹20,000 [5]. I found no update details in the sources.
- **Vivo T4x 5G:** This is the cheaper option, from ₹15,499, with a 6,500mAh battery [1][5].

**Avoid for your priorities**
- Vivo T5 Lite 5G, because it is sluggish and has heavy bloatware [5][6].
- Ai+ Nova 2 Ultra 5G, because it gets only one major OS update [5][6].
- Lava Blaze Duo 3 5G, because its OS support is limited [5][6].
- Realme P4x 5G, whose camera setup is described as weak [1][5].

**Bottom line:** The Redmi 15 5G (256GB) is the best fit for battery and value, and the update promise looks good, though the sources disagree on its length. If camera and guaranteed updates matter more than battery, compare the Samsung A17 and M17 in a shop and check the camera and update claims first.
