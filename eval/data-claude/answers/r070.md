**Recommendation: Surfshark on its 27-month Starter plan**

Surfshark is the best fit across all three of your criteria.

- **Price:** The Starter plan costs about $2.50 a month on a 27-month term [3][8]. That is well under $5.
- **Audit:** Deloitte audited its no-logs policy in 2023 and 2025 [3][8]. The sources don't say how deep those audits went. Proton's CEO notes that some competitors audit a privacy policy rather than live infrastructure [5]. Read Surfshark's reports if that distinction matters to you.
- **Streaming:** It unblocked 13 of 14 services in Gizmodo's test, including Hulu and JioHotstar. Only ITVX failed, and it needed no browser extension for any service [3].
- **Speed:** Its UK server tested at 186.96 Mbps, the best UK result Gizmodo recorded, with a 9.6% average speed loss [3]. There is no device limit [3]. Connections are sometimes slow to establish, and there is no Smart DNS for Samsung or LG TVs [3].

**Alternatives**

- **Proton VPN** is the pick if privacy matters most. It costs $2.50–$2.99 a month on a two-year plan [3][4][7]. Securitum has audited it for five consecutive years, and its apps are open source [2][5]. It unblocked 10 of 14 services but missed Hulu, ITVX and Pluto TV, with a 10.2% average speed loss [3]. Sources disagree on the 2026 audit's scope. CyberInsider says it covered both free and paid servers [2]. The Bandwidth Ledger says it sampled only free-tier servers [8]. Check Proton's published report.
- **NordVPN** is the strongest for streaming. It unblocked all 14 services, lost 7.8% of speed on average, and has six independent audits [3]. Its price is disputed. Gizmodo, Tom's Guide and a cost guide put it at about $3.49–$3.50 a month on a two-year plan [3][4][7]. The Bandwidth Ledger says a 2026 pricing change moved it to $5.99 [8]. Confirm the checkout price first. It also has the steepest renewal increase [7][8].
- **ExpressVPN** also unblocked all 14 services and is under $3 a month on a 28-month term. Its no-logs policy is independently audited, but it renews at a much higher price [3].
- **PrivadoVPN** is the cheapest at about $1.11 a month, and its speeds are reportedly close to NordVPN's and Surfshark's [4][8]. Only The Bandwidth Ledger says it completed an audit in 2026 [8]. Tom's Guide's older guide says it had no independent audit [4]. I'd treat that claim as unconfirmed.
- **Skip for this brief:** Mullvad charges a flat rate of about $5.50 a month [7]. DuckDuckGo's VPN is audited but costs $9.99 a month and has no streaming-optimized servers [1].

**Buying tips**

Renewals usually cost two to three times the introductory rate [7]. Check the renewal price before you pay and note the date. Black Friday deals are already live, so a long plan is cheapest now [7].
