**Recommendation: Bitwarden Families, with 1Password Families as the paid alternative**

For a family of four on iPhone, Android and Windows, I recommend Bitwarden Families. It has the strongest match with your three priorities: audits, track record and price.

**Why Bitwarden**
- **Audits:** Bitwarden's code is open source, and one source says it has been audited several times by independent firms [5]. Another says it publishes Cure53 audit results [6]. A third lists SOC 2 Type 2 and published third-party audits [8].
- **Platforms:** It has iOS and Android apps and Windows desktop apps [2]. Its Families plan covers six users, so four people fit [8].
- **Price:** It is among the cheapest options, at roughly $40–48 a year for the whole family. The sources disagree on the exact figure, with $40 in [2] and [5] and $47.88 in [8]. [8] checked vendor pricing pages in September 2026, so I would trust that figure more, but confirm it before buying. There is also a free tier with unlimited devices [2][8].
- **Weak points:** The interface is less polished than 1Password's [2][5].

**Caveats on the audit evidence**
Only [2] claims a specific January 2026 Cure53 audit with zero critical vulnerabilities. That page also makes some unusual claims, such as quantum-resistant encryption, and its comparison table is hard to read. Treat that audit claim as unconfirmed until you check Bitwarden's own audit page. The broader point that Bitwarden is audited is supported by several sources [5][6][8].

**Alternatives**
- **1Password Families:** [8] calls it the best overall and most polished option. Its audits include Cure53 penetration tests, SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 [8]. Its Secret Key design means a server breach alone would not expose a vault [8]. It costs $5.99 a month for five people with no free tier [8], which is about $72 a year [2]. That is the better pick if you will pay for polish.
- **NordPass and RoboForm:** These are cheaper, but their prices vary a lot between sources. RoboForm's family plan is $19.10 a year in [1] but $47.75 a year in [8]. These look like promotional versus list prices, which matters at renewal. Both are described as third-party audited, but with little detail [1][8]. RoboForm is also described as having had no breaches in 25 years [1][4]. [1] and [4] are rankings with coupon links, so I weight them lower on security. [1] ranks RoboForm first, while [8] puts 1Password and Bitwarden ahead for families.
- **Avoid LastPass:** Its 2022 breach exposed encrypted vaults and unencrypted URLs [6][8]. The UK regulator fined it £1.2 million [8]. A June 2026 incident exposed customer contact data but reportedly not vaults [3]. [3] comes from competitor Keeper's marketing blog, so I rely on it only for the facts LastPass itself confirmed.

**Uncertainty:** Most of these sources are review sites with affiliate or marketing incentives. Prices change often. Before you buy, check the current price and the latest audit reports on each vendor's own site.
