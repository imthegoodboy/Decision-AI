**Recommendation: NordVPN (Basic plan, two-year term)**

NordVPN is the option that best satisfies all three of your criteria at once: audited no-logs claims, strong streaming speeds, and a long-term price under $5 a month.

**Audits.** Gizmodo notes that six independent audits since 2018 back NordVPN's no-log policy [3].

**Streaming and speed.** In Gizmodo's tests, NordVPN unblocked all 14 streaming services tried, a result only ExpressVPN matched. Its US server hit 181.50 Mbps on a 190.36 Mbps connection, and average speed loss across UK, US and Japanese servers was 7.8%, which leaves far more speed than 4K needs [3]. Comparitech's August 2026 benchmark also placed NordVPN among the fastest providers, alongside Mullvad and ExpressVPN [6]. Caveats: Pluto TV and Prime Video occasionally detected it, though both worked on the next attempt [3].

**Price.** The Basic plan is under $3.50 a month on a two-year term [3]. Another guide lists it at about $3.50 on a two-year-plus-three-months plan, against about $15 month to month [7]. Be careful at renewal. Gizmodo says NordVPN's renewal increase is the largest among the VPNs it compared, and tax may be added at checkout [7]. Note the renewal date when you subscribe.

**Alternatives**

- **Proton VPN** is the pick if privacy matters most. It has passed five consecutive annual no-logs audits by Securitum, the latest in May 2026 [2][5], and its VPN Plus plan costs under $3 a month on two years [3]. It unblocked 10 of 14 streaming services, but not Hulu or ITVX [3]. Its average speed loss was 10.2% [3], and Comparitech's benchmark scored it lower on speed [6].
- **ExpressVPN** also unblocked all 14 services with the same 7.8% average speed loss. Its audited no-log policy and under-$3 Basic price on a 28-month term are attractive, but it renews at a much higher price [3].
- **Surfshark** unblocked 13 of 14 services, though not ITVX. Deloitte audited its no-log policy in 2023 and 2025, and the Starter plan is about $2.50 a month on 27 months [3].

**What to skip.** DuckDuckGo's VPN passed an independent audit, but it costs $9.99 a month and has no streaming-optimized servers [1]. PrivadoVPN is very cheap and good for streaming, but Tom's Guide says it has not had an independent audit [4]. IPVanish completed a third no-logs audit in September 2026 [8], but it did not appear in Gizmodo's streaming ranking [3].

**Bottom line.** Buy NordVPN's two-year Basic plan for the best streaming results, with audited privacy and a low monthly price. Choose Proton VPN if audit transparency matters more than unblocking every streaming service. Use the refund window to test your own services.
