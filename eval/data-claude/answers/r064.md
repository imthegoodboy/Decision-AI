**Recommendation: NordPass Family, with 1Password Families as the pick if audit transparency matters more than price.**

**Why NordPass**
- It runs on iPhone, Android and Windows, plus all major browsers [7][4].
- The Family plan gives six separate premium accounts, each with a private vault. That covers a family of four with room to spare [4][7].
- Each member also gets item sharing, password health reports, breach scanning, email masking and emergency access [7].
- It uses XChaCha20 encryption with a zero-knowledge design, so NordPass can't read your vaults [4][7]. Its security architecture has been independently audited [1][7].
- It is cheap. The Family plan is about $3.69 a month on a one-year term and about $2.39 on a two-year term [4][7]. Another review lists a higher list price of $6.24 a month [4].
- I found no breach or regulatory action against NordPass in these sources.

**Caveats**
- The sources say "audited" but give little detail on who audited NordPass or when. That is weaker than the published, named audits for 1Password and Bitwarden. Ask for the report before you commit.
- NordPass raises its price at renewal, so budget for year two [1][7]. Item sharing is also clunkier than 1Password's shared vaults [4][7].
- Several of these reviews carry coupon or affiliate links [4]. Treat the "best" rankings with some caution.

**Alternatives**
- **1Password Families (five users):** This is the strongest on verifiable security. Its Secret Key design means a full server compromise still wouldn't expose your vault. It publishes Cure53 penetration test results and holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 [7][9]. It has no free tier, and the price is the catch. List prices run from $5.99 a month [9] to a $9.99 renewal [7], so it is the priciest mainstream option.
- **RoboForm Family (five users):** This is the cheapest, at about $1.59 a month promotional or $47.75 a year list [1][9]. It has SOC 2 Type 2, a passed third-party audit and no reported breach in about 25 years [7]. Its interface looks dated [4][7].
- **Bitwarden:** It is open source and has a free tier, but the sources disagree. One says the Families plan covers six users at $40 to $48 a year [2][9]. Another says it caps at three users and reports that a January 2026 Cure53 audit found two critical autofill-isolation flaws [7]. A third says the same audit passed with zero critical vulnerabilities [2]. Check Bitwarden's own audit report and plan terms before choosing it.
- **Keeper:** It is the most heavily certified, with SOC 2, ISO 27001 and FedRAMP High for its government cloud [3][7]. But it costs $91.99 a year for five users and is aimed at IT departments [7].

**Avoid LastPass.** Its 2022 breach exposed encrypted vaults and unencrypted URLs, and the UK ICO fined it £1.2 million [7][9]. A June 2026 third-party incident then exposed customer contact data, though not vaults [3].

**Setup tips:** Give each person their own vault. Create one shared folder for streaming and household logins. Turn on emergency access for both parents. Have everyone choose a long, unique master password [7].
