**Recommendation: NordVPN on its two-year Basic plan, with one caveat about the audit evidence.**

**Why NordVPN**
- **Streaming:** In Gizmodo's tests it unblocked all 14 streaming services tried. Only ExpressVPN matched that [3]. Its US server hit 181.50 Mbps on a 190.36 Mbps line, and average speed loss was 7.8% [3]. Comparitech's Q3 2026 benchmark also placed it among the three fastest providers [6].
- **Price:** The Basic plan is under $3.50 a month on a two-year term [3]. Gizmodo's pricing guide gives about $3.50 on 2 years plus 3 months [7].
- **Audits:** Gizmodo says six independent audits since 2018 back its no-log policy [3]. I could not find an audit report for NordVPN in these sources. The count rests on that one article. TechRadar [8] and Gizmodo [7] only say generally that NordVPN has repeat audits or a long privacy record.

**Price caveats:** Long-plan prices are introductory. Renewals usually run two to three times the first-term rate, and NordVPN's rises the most. Sales tax can also be added at checkout [7].

**Alternatives**
- **Proton VPN** has the best-documented audit trail. Securitum has audited it five years running, most recently in May 2026, with the report published [2][5]. The audit covers only a sample of servers at one point in time [2]. It costs about $2.50–$3 a month on two years [3][7], but it is weaker for streaming. Gizmodo found it unblocked 10 of 14 services, failing Hulu, ITVX and Pluto TV, with 10.2% average speed loss [3]. Comparitech ranked it near the bottom of its speed chart, with high EU latency [6].
- **Surfshark** is about $2.50 a month on 27 months. It unblocked 13 of 14 services, and Deloitte audited it in 2023 and 2025 [3]. Its average speed loss was 9.6% [3]. Comparitech's scoring rows for it are truncated, so I can't confirm its rank [6].
- **ExpressVPN** is under $3 a month on 28 months. It also unblocked all 14 services, and its no-log policy is independently audited [3]. Its renewal price is high [3].

**Where sources disagree**
Tom's Guide says Proton unblocked "every streaming site" and had "very impressive" speeds [4]. Gizmodo and Comparitech both report weaker results [3][6]. I trust the newer, more specific tests. The Tom's Guide page lists a 2022 publish date [4], and Gizmodo's October 2026 test names the services that failed [3]. Gizmodo's tests are also a single reviewer's, and Comparitech's numbers come from its own benchmark hosts, not your connection [6].

**Not recommended for this brief**
- DuckDuckGo VPN is audited but costs $9.99 a month and lacks streaming-optimised servers [1].
- PrivadoVPN is very cheap but has not been independently audited [4].
- Mullvad tested fastest [6] but costs about $5.50 a month on every plan [7], which is over your limit.

If audit transparency matters more to you than Hulu or ITVX access, choose Proton. Otherwise choose NordVPN.
