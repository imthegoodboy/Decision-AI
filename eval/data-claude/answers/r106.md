I recommend **NordVPN** on its two-year Basic plan. It's the only option in these sources that meets all three of your criteria with room to spare.

**Audited no-logs claims.** Gizmodo says six independent audits since 2018 back NordVPN's no-log policy [3]. Gizmodo's pros list also calls it a "repeatedly audited no-log policy" [3].

**Streaming speeds.** In Gizmodo's tests, NordVPN unblocked all 14 streaming services, a result only ExpressVPN matched [3]. Its US server hit 181.50 Mbps on a 190.36 Mbps connection, the fastest US result Gizmodo recorded. Average speed loss was 7.8%, which leaves far more than 4K needs [3]. Comparitech's Q3 2026 benchmark agrees: it named NordVPN among the three fastest providers [6]. NordVPN also has Smart DNS for TVs and consoles [3].

**Price.** The Basic plan is about $3.50 a month on the two-year-plus-three-months term, paid up front [3][7]. That is well under your $5 limit. Renewal is the catch. Gizmodo says most VPNs renew at two to three times the introductory rate, and NordVPN increases the most [7]. Check the renewal price before you buy, and turn off auto-renew if you only want the first term [7].

**Alternatives**
- **Proton VPN** is the best pick if privacy comes first. Securitum has audited its no-logs policy five years running, most recently in May 2026, and it publishes the full reports [2][5]. It costs about $2.50 to $3 a month on a two-year plan [4][7]. Its streaming is weaker. Gizmodo found it unblocked 10 of 14 services, missing Hulu, ITVX and Pluto TV, and its average speed loss was 10.2% [3]. Comparitech's data also showed slower speed retention than NordVPN [6].
- **ExpressVPN** also unblocked all 14 services, with the same 7.8% speed loss. Its Basic plan is under $3 a month on a 28-month term, and its no-log policy is independently audited [3]. It renews at a much higher price [3].
- **Surfshark** unblocked 13 of 14 services (not ITVX) and costs about $2.50 a month on a 27-month term. Deloitte audited its no-log policy in 2023 and 2025, and one subscription covers unlimited devices [3].

**What to avoid.** DuckDuckGo VPN passed a Securitum audit, but it lacks streaming-optimized servers and costs $9.99 a month [1]. PrivadoVPN is very cheap and fast, but Tom's Guide says it has not undergone an independent audit [4].

**Caveats.** Audits are point-in-time reviews of sampled servers, not continuous guarantees [2]. Streaming services also change their VPN detection often. NordVPN, ExpressVPN and Surfshark all had occasional failures in Gizmodo's tests [3]. Use the refund window to test your own streaming services before committing.
