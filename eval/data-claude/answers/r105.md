## Recommendation
Choose **Bitwarden Families**. It scored highest in the evidence-weighted ranking (71.5/100), narrowly ahead of 1Password (68) and RoboForm (68). Confidence is only moderate because the sources say nothing verified about Bitwarden's breach history. The family plan covers 6 users, which is enough for four people, and the apps run on iPhone, Android and Windows.

## Why
- **Audits are published.** Bitwarden publishes its Cure53 audit results [6]. Its code is open source and has been audited multiple times by independent security firms [5].
- **Price.** It has an unlimited free tier [8][2][5]. A Families plan is listed at $47.88/yr for 6 users, with SOC 2 Type 2 and published third-party audits [8].
- **Platforms.** Desktop apps exist for Windows [2]. Source 2 also lists iOS and Android mobile apps, but only partly in the passage I checked, so treat mobile support as likely rather than fully verified.
- **LastPass is out.** Its 2022-23 breach stole encrypted vault data [6][3][8]. The UK ICO fined it £1.2 million [8]. A June 2026 incident exposed customer information but, per LastPass, not vaults [3].

## Trade-offs
- Bitwarden's interface is described as less polished than 1Password's [5].
- None of the retrieved sources verifies Bitwarden's own breach record, which is a gap on your top priority.
- Most sources are review or blog sites. Some are vendor-run or use coupon links, so treat them as moderately reliable.

## Alternatives
- **1Password:** Regular independent audits including Cure53, plus SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 [8]. Its Secret Key never leaves your devices [8]. Families costs $5.99/month for five people, with no free tier [8], so it is the priciest main option. Choose it if you want polish and will pay for it.
- **RoboForm:** Cheapest option, with a family plan of up to five accounts at about $1.59/month promo [4][1]. Two sources report no breach in 25 years [1][4], but I recorded no verified audit claim for it.
- **NordPass:** Six premium accounts [7][1][8], with a third-party audit reported but no auditor named [8]. Renewal prices rise [1].
- **Keeper:** The "never breached" claim comes only from Keeper's own blog [3], so I would not rely on it.

## Conflicting evidence
- **Bitwarden family price:** Source 8 says $47.88/yr [8]. Sources 2 and 5 say about $40/yr [2][5]. Source 8 says its prices were verified in September 2026 against vendor pages, and source 2 is dated 2023 despite its "2026" title. After verification, source 2's reliability score fell and source 8's rose slightly, so $47.88 is more likely, but it is unconfirmed. Either figure is far cheaper than 1Password.
- **NordPass price:** Source 1 quotes $2.79/month and source 4 lists $6.24/month before coupons [1][4]. Promotional and list prices differ.

## Before you decide
- Confirm current prices on each vendor's site.
- Read Bitwarden's latest Cure53 report and its incident history.
- Start with the free tier, then upgrade to Families.
- Give everyone a strong master password. Vault safety depends on it [6].
