## Recommendation
Sony ULT WEAR is the best-evidenced over-ear for your commute, but the sources give no UK price, so confirm it is under £200 before buying. Confidence is moderate, because most of the evidence is a single review roundup.

## Why
- SoundGuys names it the best overall pick under $200. It credits effective ANC in a compact, foldable over-ear design [6].
- In lab testing it gave more than 25dB of attenuation above 80Hz, which SoundGuys calls well suited to commutes [6].
- It also earned SoundGuys' Best ANC and Best for Comfort recognition [6].
- ANC works best on steady low-frequency noise such as train rumble [5]. Lightweight design and soft cushions can matter as much as ANC strength on long train journeys [5].

## Trade-offs
- It is bass-forward by default. SoundGuys recommends using the app EQ to tame the bass boost [6]. This matters less to you, since bass is a low priority.
- The SoundGuys list is priced in dollars. The sources do not state a £ price for the ULT WEAR, so I could not verify it fits your budget.
- The comfort and ANC claims come from one outlet.

## Alternatives
- **Sony WH-1000XM5.** This is the strongest on quality. What Hi-Fi? places it among the class leaders for isolating train and Underground drone [1]. It also says comfort is up there with the best, with just the right clamping force [1]. The evidence-weighted ranking put it first for that reason. I still do not lead with it because it launched at £380 [1], well over your limit. What Hi-Fi? says it has been superseded by the XM6 and that discounts are likely [1], but gives no price. Choose it only if you find it under £200. The review dates from 2022.
- **Soundcore Space 2.** Anker calls it the best choice if comfort and battery life are your priorities [5]. That is vendor-only evidence, and I found no independent ANC data for it.
- **JBL Live 650BTNC (£179.99 in a 2019 listing).** Its ANC is good but not a patch on Bose or Sony [2]. That review is old.
- Avoid the JBL Live 670NC for your needs. It is on-ear, not over-ear [6].
- The Sennheiser Accentum has effective ANC according to one source [4]. That source gives only a dollar price and no measurements.

## Conflicting evidence
The sources do not directly contradict each other. They differ in age and independence. The 2019 Ideal Home and 2022 What Hi-Fi? reviews are dated, and Anker's guide is a manufacturer's own marketing. SoundGuys is the most recent independent source with lab measurements, so I weighted it most on ANC. It is still a single source.

## Before you decide
- Check current UK prices for the ULT WEAR and for a discounted XM5.
- Try them on if you can. You will wear them for 90 minutes a day, so fit matters more than any review score.
- Look for a second independent ANC measurement of the ULT WEAR.
