**Recommendation: Marcus by Goldman Sachs High-Yield Savings**

Marcus fits your three priorities most cleanly. One source calls it a "genuinely clean proposition" with no monthly fee, no minimum opening deposit, no minimum balance to earn the full APY, and no cap on withdrawals or transfers [2]. It also allows same-day transfers on amounts up to $100,000, and you can link external accounts. The app is described as user-friendly, and customer service is available 24/7 [4]. Its 3.50% APY wasn't the highest listed in May 2026 [2], but it requires nothing beyond opening and funding the account [2]. For an emergency fund, that predictability matters more than chasing a promotional rate.

Two cautions:
- **No ATM access or checking account.** Marcus has neither, so it works best as a dedicated savings account paired with your existing checking account [2].
- **FDIC status.** The sources describe high-yield savings accounts generally as federally insured deposit accounts [2], and FDIC coverage runs up to $250,000 per depositor [4]. That covers $15,000 easily. None of the sources states Marcus's FDIC status explicitly, so confirm the "Member FDIC" notice on its site before you fund it.

**Alternatives**
- **Vio Bank (4.00% APY).** It pays that rate on all balances with no monthly activity required and no monthly fees. It requires a $100 opening deposit and has no checking account or debit card, so you must transfer money out to spend it [6].
- **EverBank (3.90% APY).** It has no opening minimum, no monthly fees, and no required monthly behavior. Its app is rated 4.7 stars [6].
- **Synchrony (3.40% APY).** It has no fees or minimums and offers an optional ATM card [2][4].
- **Discover Online Savings.** It has no monthly fee, no minimum balance, and no transaction limit [4]. The source gives no rate for it.
- **Ally.** An older review (2017) praises its transfers and 24/7 support, but it lists a $10 fee after six withdrawals in a month [5]. Verify current terms.

**Accounts I would skip for this purpose**
- **Axos (4.21% in one source, 5.00% in another).** The two sources disagree on the rate. The top rate requires direct deposits and a minimum balance, and it drops to 1.00% if you miss them [2][6].
- **SoFi and LendingClub.** Their headline 4.00% rates depend on promotions or monthly deposit requirements [2].
- **Varo.** Its high rate applies only to the first $5,000 of your balance [6].

**Bottom line:** Rates are variable and the figures above come from articles dated May to July 2026 [2][6]. Check current APYs before you open an account. If you want a higher rate with the same simplicity, Vio or EverBank are the closest alternatives. If you want the least conditions and the fastest transfers, choose Marcus.
