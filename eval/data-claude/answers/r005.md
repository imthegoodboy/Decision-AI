**Recommendation: Redmi 15 5G (256GB), with one caveat about its update promise**

**Why it fits your priorities**
- **Battery:** It has a 7,000mAh battery, which Croma calls one of the biggest in the segment [1]. Samsung's Galaxy A17 5G and M17 5G have 5,000mAh cells [4].
- **Price:** It starts at ₹15,999 [1][5], which leaves room for the 256GB version. Both Croma and The Rupee Gadget Desk suggest that version for a growing photo library [1][5].
- **Camera:** It has 50MP dual rear cameras and an 8MP front camera [1]. None of the sources tests its low-light photos, so I can't promise how it handles kids in a dim room.

**Where the sources disagree**
- **Redmi 15 5G updates:** Croma (Feb 2026) says 2 years of OS updates and 4 years of security updates [1]. The Rupee Gadget Desk (Nov 2026) says 4 years of OS updates and 6 years of security updates [5]. I can't settle this from these sources, so plan on the weaker figure. I would also trust [5] a little less because it conflicts with Samsung's own page on two other points.
- **Galaxy M17 5G updates:** [5] says 2 OS upgrades and 4 years of security patches. Samsung says 6 OS generations and 6 years of security updates [4]. A manufacturer's own spec is usually more reliable, though it is marketing.
- **Galaxy A17 5G camera:** [5] says the main camera has no optical image stabilisation (OIS). Samsung lists a 50MP main camera with OIS [4]. I'd trust Samsung on its own spec.

**Alternatives**
- **Galaxy A17 5G:** Choose this if updates and indoor photos matter more than battery. Croma says it has "one of the best software update promises in this segment" and good cameras, but gives no figures [1]. Samsung lists OIS [4]. Its battery is smaller, and none of the sources give its price.
- **Galaxy M17 5G:** Samsung lists OIS, 6 OS upgrades and 25W charging [4]. The sources give no price for it, so check that it is under ₹20,000.
- **Oppo K13 5G:** It has a 7,000mAh battery and 80W charging, and starts at ₹19,999 [1][5]. Digit lists the K13 at ₹24,999 [2], so check the price for the version you want.
- **Vivo T4x 5G:** It has a 6,500mAh battery and starts at ₹15,499 [1].

**Avoid**
- **Vivo T5 Lite 5G:** Beebom found it slow and full of bloatware [6].
- **Galaxy A07 5G:** It promises 6 years of updates but has a 720p LCD and soft selfies [6].

**Confidence:** I'm confident about the battery sizes and prices, which the sources agree on. I'm unsure about update lengths and low-light photo quality. Before buying, check the manufacturer's current update policy for the exact model. If possible, also look at real indoor sample photos from the Redmi 15 5G and Galaxy A17 5G.
