**Recommendation: Sony ULT WEAR, with a price check first**

SoundGuys picks the Sony ULT WEAR as its best overall pair under $200. It credits them with effective ANC, a 32 hour 35 minute lab battery result and more than 25dB of attenuation above 80Hz, which it calls "well suited for commutes and travel". It also says they earned its Best ANC and Best for Comfort recognition [6]. SoundGuys measures ANC on a test head and also wears each pair day to day to judge comfort [6]. That makes it the best-supported source here for your two priorities.

Two caveats:
- **Price:** SoundGuys prices in dollars. I found no source giving a UK price for the ULT WEAR, so confirm it is under £200 before buying [6].
- **Bass:** SoundGuys says they are bass-forward and suggests using the app EQ to tame the ULT button's bass boost [6]. You care less about bass, so this is a small annoyance, not a dealbreaker.

Everything above about the ULT WEAR comes from this one source. I found no second review to confirm it.

**Alternatives**
- **Soundcore Space 2:** Anker describes it as lightweight, with soft cushions, a pressure-relieving headband and up to 50 hours of battery with ANC on [5]. This is the best pick if comfort is the priority. However, Anker sells it and the page compares only Anker models, so these are manufacturer claims, not independent tests [5]. I found no lab ANC data for it.
- **Sennheiser Accentum ($189.95):** Major HiFi calls its ANC "effective" and its battery long, but it is bass-forward and the page gives no measurements [4]. Treat it as weak evidence.
- **Sony WH-1000XM5:** What Hi-Fi? says it is comfortable and among the class leaders at isolating train and Underground drone. It also says the Bose QuietComfort Ultra cuts out even more noise [1]. It launched at £380, and the review only says discounts are likely [1]. I cannot confirm it is under £200, and it has since been superseded by the XM6 [1].
- **JBL Live 670NC:** SoundGuys measured ANC approaching 30dB around 250Hz, but it is an on-ear design, not over-ear [6].

**Source disagreements and reliability**
- Ideal Home calls the Sony WH-1000XM3 the best you can buy. The article is dated 2019 and lists prices like £259.99, so despite its "2026" heading it is outdated. I give it little weight [2].
- That same article rates the JBL Live 650BTNC (£179.99) as "not a patch on the Bose or Sony" for ANC, with audible hiss when no music is playing. It is also dated, so I would not rely on it [2].
- The Sony WF-1000XM5 review covers earbuds, not over-ears, so I ignored it [3].
- The Which? article is from 2019 and the RTINGS page has no useful comparison data, so I did not use either [7][8].

**Overall confidence:** moderate for the ULT WEAR on noise cancelling, and weaker on comfort, since that rests on SoundGuys alone. UK pricing is unverified for every pair. If you can, try the ULT WEAR and Space 2 in a shop, because comfort over 90 minutes varies from person to person.
