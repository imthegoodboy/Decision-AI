## Recommendation

**Surfshark** on its long-term Starter plan. It had the best verified mix of audited no-logs claims, streaming performance and price. The evidence-weighted ranking tied it with NordVPN at 82.5/100. I chose Surfshark because its evidence was stronger (97% confidence vs 75%) and NordVPN's price is disputed.

## Why

- Deloitte audited Surfshark's no-log policy in 2023 and 2025 [3]. A second source repeats this [8].
- It unblocked 13 of 14 streaming services in Gizmodo's tests, with no browser extension needed. ITVX was its only failure [3]. Its average speed loss was 9.6%, which is fine for streaming [3].
- It costs about $2.50 a month on a 27-month Starter plan, well under $5 [3][8].

## Trade-offs

- It could not unblock ITVX, and connections sometimes take a while to establish [3].
- It has no Smart DNS, so Samsung and LG TVs need a router setup [3].
- Deloitte's audit is confirmed by two sources, but neither source says what the audit covered. I could not check that.
- The streaming results come from one reviewer's tests, so treat them as indicative [3].

## Alternatives

- **ExpressVPN:** unblocked all 14 services with a 7.8% average speed loss [3]. It costs under $3 a month on a 28-month term [3]. Its no-log policy is described as independently audited, but the auditor is not named [3]. It renews at a much higher price [3].
- **NordVPN:** unblocked all 14 services [3] and has six independent audits since 2018 [3]. Comparitech's Q3 2026 lab tests also rated it among the fastest [6]. The two-year Basic plan is under $3.50 a month per Gizmodo [3][7] (see conflict below).
- **Proton VPN:** five consecutive annual Securitum audits [2][5]. It is under $3 a month on a two-year plan [3], but unblocked only 10 of 14 services, missing Hulu and ITVX [3].
- **Not recommended on this brief:**
  - DuckDuckGo VPN passed an audit [1], but costs $9.99 a month and lacks streaming-optimized servers [1].
  - Mullvad charges a flat rate of about $5.50 [7], which is over budget.
  - PrivadoVPN is cheapest at about $1.11 a month [8], but its audit status is unverified (see below).

## Conflicting evidence

- **NordVPN price:** Gizmodo says about $3.50 a month [3][7] and Tom's Guide says $3.49 [4]. The Bandwidth Ledger says $5.99 after a "2026 pricing overhaul" [8]. I trust the three agreeing sources more. The Bandwidth Ledger scored lowest for reliability in the tool's check. If it is right, NordVPN fails the $5 limit.
- **Proton audit scope:** CyberInsider says the 2026 audit covered both free and paid servers [2]. The Bandwidth Ledger says only free-tier servers were sampled [8]. The tool marked both claims contradicted because neither is corroborated, so I could not resolve this. CyberInsider cites the audit report, so it is probably more reliable. Even so, treat Proton's paid-server coverage as unconfirmed.
- **PrivadoVPN audit:** the Bandwidth Ledger says it completed its first audit in early 2026 [8]. An older Tom's Guide piece says it had no audit [4]. The audit claim rests on that one source, so I did not rely on it.

## Before you decide

- Check the renewal price at checkout. Long plans often renew at two to three times the intro rate [7].
- Test your own streaming services during the refund window.
- Audits are point-in-time reviews, not continuous guarantees. Securitum said this about Proton's audit [2].
- Most sources are trade press with affiliate links, and the streaming tests come from a single reviewer.
