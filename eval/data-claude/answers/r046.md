**Recommendation: Eureka Forbes 355 (about ₹9,999)**

This is my pick for a 350 sq ft Delhi bedroom. Both of the most relevant sources list it as a 355 m³/h CADR unit with a HEPA H13, pre-filter and carbon stack [1][2]. A common rule of thumb puts the minimum CADR at roughly 235 m³/h for this room. For Delhi, one source says to aim for 300 m³/h or more so the room's air cycles three to four times an hour [2]. At 355 m³/h it clears that bar with headroom, and it costs well under your ₹15,000 cap [1][2].

**Caveats**
- **Noise:** It is rated at 63 dB, which is loud for a bedroom, where under 30 dB is the comfort zone [1][2]. Plan to run it on a low or night setting while you sleep. On bad smog days, run it on medium or high for an hour first, then drop the speed [2][3].
- **Plasma stage:** The spec sheet lists a plasma filter [1]. Ionising features can produce ozone [1][2], so check whether you can switch it off.
- **Filter cost:** I found no replacement-filter price for this model. The sources only give a general range of ₹1,500 to ₹5,000 a year, and advise confirming local availability before you buy [1][2]. Check that for the Eureka Forbes 355 first, since you said filter cost matters most.

**Alternatives**
- **Realme TechLife (₹7,999, often ₹7,499):** One source makes it the top pick, with 330 m³/h and a "True HEPA H13" filter made by Sharp [2]. However, an older review of the same unit says it uses a HEPA H12 filter that removes 99.95% of particles, and it is also loud at 64 dB [7]. The two sources disagree on the filter grade, so I ranked it second given your true-HEPA priority.
- **Qubo Q600 (₹14,490):** It has 450 m³/h and H13 filtration, but it is built for large open-plan areas and runs at about 55 dB [1][2]. It is the choice if you want the most power and can pay near the cap.
- **Honeywell Air Touch V5 (₹10,199):** It is very quiet at about 22 dB and has H13 filtration [1][2]. The sources disagree on its CADR: 380 m³/h in one [1] and 250 m³/h in the other, which calls it undersized for this room [2]. Check the CADR on the manufacturer's listing before considering it.
- **Mi Air Purifier 4:** It has a high CADR (387 m³/h) and is quiet at about 32 dB, but its price differs by source [1][2]. One source lists ₹14,999 on Flipkart and ₹17,999 on Amazon [1]. The other lists ₹17,999 on both [2]. It only fits your budget if you catch a sale.

**Avoid for this room:** the Qubo Q200 (150 m³/h, rated for about 200 sq ft) [2][6] and the Levoit Core 300, whose CADR is low for Delhi winters [2].

**Buying tips:** Insist on true HEPA H13 and avoid "HEPA-type" labels [1][3]. Also keep the unit at least a foot from walls and keep windows closed while it runs [1][2].

Note that Digit's list is based on advertised specifications, not hands-on testing [1].
