**Recommendation: Sony ULT WEAR, with caveats about price and bass.**

SoundGuys, which lab-tests with an artificial head, picks it as its best overall under $200. It also credits it with Best ANC, Best for Comfort and Best Over-Ear Headphones. It measured more than 25dB of attenuation above 80Hz and lasted 32 hours 35 minutes [6]. That matches your priorities of comfort and noise cancellation. I could not confirm a UK price in the sources, because the SoundGuys price table is in dollars and is garbled in the retrieved text [6]. Check the current £ price before buying. The ULT WEAR is also bass-heavy, and SoundGuys recommends using the app EQ to tame the ULT button's bass boost [6]. That matters less to you, but it is a setting to change.

**Where sources disagree**
- **ULT WEAR noise cancelling:** Platform & Pocket says its low-frequency attenuation "barely reaches 15dB" [8]. SoundGuys reports more than 25dB above 80Hz [6]. The two quote the same battery figure, so [8] seems to draw on the same testing, yet its 15dB number does not appear in [6]. I trust SoundGuys, the primary lab source with a described method. This is still a real conflict, so treat the ANC as good but not proven.
- **JBL Live 670NC:** [8] names it the top pick and calls it a "full over-ear design" with cushions that don't press. SoundGuys calls it an "on-ear form factor" [6]. You asked for over-ear, and the comfort claim in [8] has no support in [6]. Its measured ANC is strong, approaching 30dB around 250Hz [6]. I would not recommend it for comfort unless you try it on first. Which? notes that not all on-ear headphones are comfortable for long sessions [7], although that article is from 2019.
- **Sony WH-1000XM5 weight:** [8] says about 320g, but What Hi-Fi? lists 250g [1]. I trust [1] because it is a hands-on review with a stated weight.

**Alternatives**
- **Sony WH-1000XM5:** Comfort is "up there with the best" and the ANC is class-leading on trains and the Underground [1]. It launched at £380 [1]. Only [8] suggests discounts could approach £200, and I couldn't verify that. Bose's QuietComfort Ultra cancels more noise but costs £450 [1][8].
- **Anker Soundcore Space One:** SoundGuys picked it as best for Android users. It has adjustable ANC, a foldable lightweight design and 42 hours 57 minutes of battery [6]. The sources do not explicitly call it over-ear.
- **Soundcore Space 2:** Anker's own blog says it is the comfort pick, with a lightweight design and soft cushions [5]. That is a manufacturer's page and I found no independent test, so treat it cautiously.

**Uncertainty:** Most of the evidence for the ULT WEAR comes from a single lab source [6]. None of the sources test comfort over a 90-minute ride. If you can, try the ULT WEAR in a shop for fit before committing.
