**Recommendation: Bitwarden Families.** It is the best fit for your priorities of audit record, price and cross-platform coverage. 1Password is a strong runner-up if you will pay more for polish.

**Why Bitwarden**

- **Audits.** Bitwarden's code is open source and has been audited multiple times by independent firms [5]. One source reports a January 2026 Cure53 audit with no critical vulnerabilities [2]. That source is a lower-quality blog, so check Bitwarden's own published reports. Another review notes Bitwarden publishes its Cure53 results, unlike LastPass [6]. A third lists SOC 2 Type 2 and published third-party audits [8].
- **Price.** The Families plan covers six users, which is plenty for four people. One source lists it at $40 a year [2]. Another, checked in September 2026, lists $47.88 a year [8]. Either way it is much cheaper than 1Password Families at $5.99 a month for five users [8]. The free tier also offers unlimited devices and passwords [2].
- **Platforms.** It has native iOS and Android apps with biometric unlock and system autofill, plus Windows, macOS and Linux desktop apps [2].
- **Architecture.** It uses AES-256 with zero-knowledge design, so Bitwarden cannot read your vault [2].

**Trade-offs.** The interface is less polished than 1Password's, especially on Windows [5]. Free users get email-only support [2].

**Alternatives**

- **1Password.** Reviewers call it the best overall manager. It publishes Cure53 audit results and holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 certifications. Its Secret Key means a server breach alone cannot decrypt your vault. There is no free tier, and the first-year discount makes renewal about a third higher [8].
- **NordPass.** It has a six-user family plan with published audit coverage [8] and uses XChaCha20 encryption [4]. Promotional prices run roughly $2.39 to $3.69 a month, but renewal brings a price increase [1][4].
- **RoboForm.** It has the lowest list price, at about $1.59 a month for five users [1]. It also reports no breaches in 25 years [1] and has passed a third-party audit [1]. Its SOC 2 Type 2 status is listed in [8].
- **Keeper.** It is strong on certifications, including SOC 2, ISO 27001 and FedRAMP High [8]. It costs more, at roughly $92 a year for five users [8].

**Avoid LastPass.** Its 2022 breach exposed encrypted vaults and unencrypted URLs [6]. The UK regulator fined it £1.2 million [8]. It also suffered another incident in 2026, though vaults were reportedly unaffected [3]. Note that [3] is a competitor's blog post.

**Caveats.** Several sources are affiliate-driven rankings, and the NordPass and RoboForm prices in [1] and [4] reflect coupons or promotions. Confirm current pricing and the latest audit reports on each vendor's site. Whichever you choose, use a long, unique master password for every family member.
