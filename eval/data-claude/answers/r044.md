**Recommendation: Honeywell Air Touch V5 (about ₹10,199 on Amazon)**

Digit lists it with a CADR of 380 m³/h, a HEPA H13 filter, a pre-filter, carbon, and about 22 dB noise. It covers 589 sq ft and costs ₹10,199 [1]. Here is why it fits your bedroom:

- **CADR:** Digit's rule of thumb is a CADR of at least two-thirds of the room's area in sq ft, assuming an 8-ft ceiling [1]. For 350 sq ft that is about 233 m³/h, and the V5 clears it comfortably.
- **Filtration:** Both Digit and Smartprix say to insist on true HEPA H13 or H14 and to avoid "HEPA-type" labels [1][2].
- **Noise:** Digit says under 30 dB is generally ideal for bedrooms [1]. The V5's listed 22 dB meets that, which matters for sleeping.

**Caveats**
- Digit's picks rest on advertised specifications only, with no hands-on testing [1]. I found no independent test of the V5.
- No source gives the V5's replacement-filter price. Digit says filters generally cost ₹1,500 to ₹5,000 a year and advises checking local availability before buying [1]. Treat filter cost as unverified for this model.
- Smartprix lists the Honeywell Air Touch U2, a different and larger model, with a filter set of about ₹8,400 [2]. Do not assume the V5 costs that much. It does show that Honeywell filter costs vary widely.

**Where the sources disagree**
- **How much CADR you need:** Digit's rule gives about 233 m³/h for your room [1]. Smartprix argues that Delhi needs 3-4 air changes an hour and calls 400 m³/h "a great fit" for a 250 sq ft bedroom [2]. On that logic, 350 sq ft would call for even more. Neither is a tested standard. Smartprix's stricter guidance is reasonable for Delhi's pollution, but it points toward the higher-CADR options below.
- **Coway Airmega AIM:** Digit lists 303 m³/h [1], while a Flipkart listing says 230 cmph for the same model name [5]. I could not resolve this. It is also pricier (₹12,499 to ₹13,499) [1][5], so I would not pick it on this evidence.
- **Mi Air Purifier 4:** Digit gives 387 m³/h and a price of ₹14,999 on Flipkart but ₹17,999 on Amazon [1]. A Flipkart listing shows other Xiaomi models with different CADR figures [5]. Check the exact model before buying.

**Alternatives**
- **Qubo Q600** (₹14,490): the highest CADR here at 450 m³/h, with H13, but about 55 dB [1]. It suits you if you value Smartprix's stricter air-change logic over quietness.
- **Eureka Forbes 355** (₹9,999): 355 m³/h with True HEPA H13, but 63 dB, which Digit calls high [1].
- **Mi Air Purifier 4:** 387 m³/h and about 32 dB [1], but it is at the budget ceiling and the price is inconsistent.
- **Qubo Q200:** its 150 m³/h CADR and "up to 200 sq ft" listing [5] are too small for 350 sq ft, even though Digit pitches it for standard bedrooms [1]. Skip it.

Prices differ between Digit and retailer listings (for example the Q200 at ₹6,790 versus ₹7,490) [1][5]. Confirm current prices and filter availability before buying.
