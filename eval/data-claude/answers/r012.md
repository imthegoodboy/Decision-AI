## Recommendation

Sony ULT WEAR is my pick, with the Sony WH-1000XM5 as the better pair if you find it under £200. The evidence is thin, so treat this as a shortlist, not a certainty. The evidence-weighted ranking put the XM5 first (72 against 69). I still lead with the ULT WEAR because the XM5 launched at £380 [1] and only gets "closer to a £200 budget" with discounts [8]. The ULT WEAR was tested in a roundup of headphones under $200 [6]. No source gives its UK price.

## Why

- The ULT WEAR is a compact, foldable over-ear with effective ANC [6].
- SoundGuys gave it Best ANC and Best for Comfort recognition [6]. This is the only source for those awards.
- ANC works best on steady low-frequency noise such as train rumble [5]. That is the noise a commute produces.
- The XM5 is the stronger performer on paper. What Hi-Fi? says its comfort is "up there with the best" [1]. It also calls it among the class leaders at isolating train and Tube drone [1]. That review dates from 2022.

## Trade-offs

- The ULT WEAR is bass-forward, so you will need to tame the ULT bass boost in the app [6][8]. You said bass matters less, so this is minor.
- The XM5 only folds flat and is not as compact [1].
- Bose QuietComfort Ultra cancels even more noise than the Sonys [1][8], but it launched at £450 [8].

## Alternatives

- Sony WH-1000XM5: choose it if you see it under £200 [1][8].
- Anker Soundcore Space One: it folds, feels lightweight and has a wired cable option [6]. It has no verified ANC depth, so I can't rank it on noise cancellation.
- Sennheiser Accentum: it has effective everyday ANC and long battery life [4]. That is an editorial description with no measurements.
- JLab JBuds Lux ANC: a budget pair with respectable but not outstanding ANC, at 10–20dB under 700Hz [6].
- JBL Live 670NC: its measured ANC approaches 30dB around 250Hz [6][8]. SoundGuys calls it on-ear [6], so it doesn't fit an over-ear brief. Over-ear is generally good for comfort [7], though that source is from 2019.

## Conflicting evidence

- ULT WEAR noise cancellation: SoundGuys reports more than 25dB of attenuation above 80Hz in lab tests [6]. Platform & Pocket says it "barely reaches 15dB" [8]. I trust SoundGuys because it describes its measurement method and is the stated source of the lab figures that [8] reuses elsewhere. The tool's reliability score actually rates [8] a little higher (65 against 58), so some doubt remains.
- JBL Live 670NC form factor: [6] says on-ear and [8] says full over-ear. I favour [6] for the same reason.
- XM5 weight: [1] lists 250g and [8] says about 320g. I favour the review's spec.

## Before you decide

- Check the current UK price of the ULT WEAR and the XM5. No source gives either, and my sources cover mainly US prices.
- Try them on in a shop if you can. Comfort over 90 minutes is personal, and the ULT WEAR's comfort claim comes from a single source.
- The Anker page [5] is a vendor guide, so I used it only for general ANC principles.
