**Recommendation: Samsung Galaxy A17 5G, with the Galaxy M17 5G as a close sibling**

Software updates and indoor photos are where the A17 wins for you. Battery is the one area where it isn't the leader.

**Why the A17**
- **Updates:** Croma Unboxed says the A17 has "one of the best software update promises in this segment" [1]. Samsung's own page states the explicit numbers for the related Galaxy M17 5G: 6 generations of OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates [4]. I found no explicit figure for the A17 itself, so confirm its policy before buying. By comparison, the Redmi 15 5G promises only 2 OS updates and 4 years of security patches [1].
- **Indoor kid photos:** The A17 has a 50MP main camera with optical image stabilisation (OIS), plus a 13MP front camera [4]. OIS helps keep shots steady in dim rooms, which is where most family photos are taken. Croma also notes the A17 stands out for its cameras and AI features [1]. Samsung's page is marketing, and none of these sources has hands-on low-light tests of the A17. Treat the camera as a good bet rather than a proven winner.
- **Battery:** It has a 5,000mAh battery [4]. That is enough for a full day, but it is smaller than the 6,500 to 7,000mAh cells on rivals [1][2].
- **Display:** It has a Super AMOLED screen [4].

**Alternatives**
- **Galaxy M17 5G:** This is the Samsung to consider if the 6-year update promise matters most. It has a 50MP OIS camera, a 5,000mAh battery with 25W charging, and a tougher display with IP54 protection [4].
- **Redmi 15 5G (from ₹15,999):** This is the pick if battery matters most. It has a 7,000mAh battery, a 6.9-inch 144Hz screen and 50MP dual cameras [1]. Its update promise is much shorter, and Croma suggests the 256GB version to hold photos [1].
- **Oppo K13 5G:** It also has a 7,000mAh battery with 80W charging, a 120Hz OLED screen and a 16MP front camera. Croma lists it from ₹19,999, though Digit lists ₹24,999, so check the current price [1][2].
- **Avoid for your needs:**
  - Realme P4x: Croma calls its camera setup weak [1].
  - Galaxy A07 5G: it promises 6 years of updates, but it has a 720p LCD screen and a soft selfie camera [5].
  - Vivo T5 Lite 5G: Beebom says it is laggy and full of bloatware [5].

**Practical tips**
- Get 8GB RAM if the price allows. Samsung's page suggests 6 to 8GB for smooth multitasking [4].
- Photos and videos of kids fill storage quickly, so choose more than 128GB if you can afford it [1].
- Prices shift with bank offers [1], so check the live price. The sources don't give a firm A17 price, so make sure it is under ₹20,000 before you buy.
- Ask to see the low-light camera in a store, or read an independent review, before you commit.
