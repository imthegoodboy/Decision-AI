## Recommendation
Buy the **Qubo Q600** (Rs 14,490 on Amazon). It has a 450 m³/h CADR and an H13 HEPA filter [1]. It has the highest CADR I could verify under Rs 15,000, and CADR is your first priority. The evidence-weighted ranking also put it first (72/100, moderate confidence). The Honeywell Air Touch V5 is the close alternative if you care more about noise and price.

## Why
- 450 m³/h is the highest verified CADR in budget [1]. Smartprix says 400 m³/h suits a 250 sq ft bedroom and recommends 3-4 air changes per hour for Delhi [2]. Your room is larger, so more CADR helps.
- It is H13 HEPA [1]. Smartprix says True HEPA H13 captures 99.97% of fine particles and that "HEPA-type" labels should be avoided [2].
- It costs Rs 14,490 [1], which is under your cap. That leaves little headroom.

## Trade-offs
- Digit lists the Q600 at about 55 dB, which is loud for a bedroom [1]. That is a single advertised figure.
- I found no replacement-filter price or life for the Q600. Digit says filters generally cost Rs 1,500-5,000 a year [1]. Check availability before buying.
- Digit's picks use advertised specifications only, with no hands-on testing [1].

## Alternatives
- **Honeywell Air Touch V5**, Rs 10,199: 380 m³/h, H13, and about 22 dB [1]. The 22 dB figure comes from one source. It is the better pick for quiet sleep, but I could not verify its filter cost.
- **Eureka Forbes 355**, Rs 9,999: 355 m³/h and True HEPA H13, but noisy [1].
- **Coway Airmega AIM**, Rs 12,499: 303 m³/h, lower CADR [1].
- **Qubo Q1000** has a 600 m³/h CADR and a 15,000-hour filter life, which means low running costs [2]. No source gives its price, so it may exceed your budget. It is worth checking.
- **Mi Air Purifier 4** is Rs 14,999 on Flipkart and Rs 17,999 on Amazon [1], so it is only in budget on Flipkart.

## Conflicting evidence
The sources disagree on how much CADR you need. Digit says CADR should be at least two-thirds of the room area in sq ft [1], which is about 233 m³/h for 350 sq ft. Smartprix says 400 m³/h for a 250 sq ft bedroom [2], which is far stricter. The tool rated Digit slightly more reliable, but neither source tested anything. For Delhi's pollution I lean towards the stricter view. On that view even the Q600 is only adequate, not generous.

## Before you decide

- The sources date from Oct-Nov 2025, so prices may have changed.
- The sealed dossier file names the V5 as its pick. This answer follows the evidence-weighted ranking, which puts the Q600 first, because you rank CADR highest.
