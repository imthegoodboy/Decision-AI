## Recommendation
Take **IELTS for UKVI (Academic)** at an approved centre. It gives the widest acceptance. The evidence on speed is thin, so treat speed as unproven. If speed matters most to you, **PTE Academic UKVI** is the main alternative. The tool's evidence-weighted ranking put IELTS first (70/100), then PTE (64), then Duolingo (40). Confidence is only moderate.

## Why
- For most UK visas that need English, the Home Office accepts only a Secure English Language Test (SELT) from an approved provider [8]. A Student visa for degree-level study needs CEFR B2 [8]. Both IELTS for UKVI and PTE Academic UKVI are on the approved list [8][1][3].
- IELTS has much broader recognition. One source says it is accepted by over 13,500 organisations, against around 3,000 for PTE [7][9]. That source is IDP, which co-owns IELTS and has a commercial interest. Source 3 independently gives the same figures [3].
- The University of Sheffield accepts all IELTS qualifications for direct postgraduate entry [2].
- IELTS lets you choose paper or computer in many locations. PTE is computer-only [7].
- The UKVI versions have the same content and format as the standard tests, so ordinary practice material works [8].

## Trade-offs
No source states how fast IELTS for UKVI results arrive, so I cannot say it is fast. Pearson reported that 85.7% of standard PTE Academic candidates got results within 2 days, but that is 2018 data and not the UKVI version [4]. A duplicate Pearson account slows PTE results [4].

## Alternatives
- **PTE Academic UKVI:** choose it if speed is your top priority and your university accepts it. Check its turnaround first (see below).
- **Duolingo English Test:** use it only if your university explicitly accepts it. Some universities assess English themselves and accept non-SELT tests [8][2].

## Conflicting evidence
- **PTE UKVI speed.** An undated source says "often within 48 hours" [1]. Source 3 says UKVI results typically take 7 to 10 working days [3]. Neither is corroborated, so the tool marked both contradicted. PTE's speed advantage for visa use is unverified.
- **Duolingo status.** Source 3 says Duolingo UKVI joined the SELT list in early 2026 [3]. Source 8, checked in September 2026 and citing GOV.UK, says Duolingo is not a SELT [8]. I trust source 8 more, because it matches the other lists and source 3 is alone on this point. Its reliability fell after verification (71 to 57). Please confirm on GOV.UK.
- **Result validity.** Source 8 says SELT results are valid for two years [8]. Source 3 says IELTS UKVI results are valid for only 12 months [3]. This is single-source and contradicted, so I could not verify it. Check the validity window before booking.

## Before you decide
- Check your offer letter and GOV.UK for the required test and score.
- Book the UKVI version at an approved SELT centre [8].
- Ask the test provider for current result turnaround and validity. Most sources here are commercial, undated or old.
