## Recommendation
Choose **Bitwarden Families** (6 users, so a family of four fits). It ranked first in the evidence-weighted analysis (76/100 vs 73 for 1Password), but the margin is narrow, and 1Password is the better pick if you will pay more for polish.

## Why
- Bitwarden's code is open source and has been audited multiple times by independent security firms [5]. Another source says it publishes Cure53 audit results [6], and a third lists "published third-party audits" [8].
- It is cheap. Two sources put the Family plan at $40/year [5][2], and a free tier with unlimited passwords is reported [8][5][2].
- 1Password is the strongest alternative on audits. It publishes independent audit results, including Cure53 penetration tests, and holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 [8]. Its Secret Key never leaves your devices or reaches its servers [8], and a second source confirms it uses a Secret Key [6].
- Avoid LastPass. Its 2022-23 breach exposed encrypted vault data and unencrypted metadata [6]. The UK ICO fined it £1.2 million in November 2025 [8]. A June 2026 third-party incident reportedly left vaults untouched [3], but that account comes from a competitor.

## Trade-offs
- 1Password costs more: $5.99/month for five people (about $72/year), with no free tier [8].
- Bitwarden is described as less polished than 1Password [5].
- The sources I could verify do not explicitly state Bitwarden's breach history, so the "track record" case rests on audits and the absence of reported incidents.

## Alternatives
- **1Password Families**: best if usability and the Secret Key design matter more than price [8].
- **NordPass**: six accounts across iPhone, Android and Windows [4]. Its audit is only briefly noted as "published" [8], so the audit evidence is thinner.
- **RoboForm**: very cheap. It reports no breach in 25 years [1][4], but that is an absence of known incidents rather than proof. Its audit evidence is thin (SOC 2 Type 2 per [8]).
- **Keeper**: lists SOC 2, ISO 27001 and FedRAMP High [3][8]. Most of that evidence is the vendor's own marketing [3].

## Conflicting evidence
- **Bitwarden family price**: [5] and [2] say $40/year, but [8] says $47.88/year. [8] states it checked vendor pages in September 2026, while [2] is dated 2023 and may be stale. I treat roughly $48 as more likely, but confirm it.
- **Keeper and RoboForm family prices** also differ across sources ([1] vs [8], and [1]/[4] list vs promo prices). The low RoboForm and NordPass prices are promotional rates, so compare renewal prices.

## Before you decide
- The claim of a January 2026 Cure53 audit with zero critical findings appears only in [2], a low-reliability source dated 2023, so I do not rely on it.
- Several sources carry affiliate or vendor ties (for example [3] and [4]).
- Check Bitwarden's current price and its latest audit reports on its own site, and confirm 1Password's renewal price.
