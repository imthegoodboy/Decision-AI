## Recommendation

NordVPN on a two-year plan. It ranked first in the evidence-weighted analysis (83/100, versus 78.5 for Proton VPN). Proton VPN is a close second and has the better-verified audit record.

## Why

- Streaming: in Gizmodo's tests NordVPN unblocked all 14 services tried, a result only ExpressVPN matched [3]. Proton VPN unblocked 10 of 14 [3].
- Speed: NordVPN's average speed loss was 7.8% [3]. Proton's was 10.2% [3]. Comparitech's 2026-Q3 benchmark also names NordVPN among the three fastest providers [6].
- Price: NordVPN's Basic plan is under $3.50 a month on a two-year term [3]. Other sources give a similar figure [7][4].
- Audits: Gizmodo says six independent audits since 2018 back NordVPN's no-log policy [3].

## Trade-offs

- The six-audit claim for NordVPN comes from one source only [3]. None of the sources include the audit reports themselves, so treat it as plausible but not fully verified.
- NordVPN's renewal price rises the most among the VPNs compared [7]. Renewals are typically two to three times the introductory rate [7]. The under-$5 figure applies to the first term only.

## Alternatives

- Proton VPN has the strongest audit evidence. Its fifth consecutive annual Securitum audit is reported by two sources [2][5]. It costs under $3 a month on a two-year term [3]. It is weaker for streaming, and it did not unblock Hulu or ITVX [3]. The audit is a point-in-time review of selected servers, not a continuous guarantee [2]. Choose it if audit transparency matters more than streaming coverage.
- Surfshark unblocked 13 of 14 services and was audited by Deloitte in 2023 and 2025, according to one source [3]. Gizmodo prices it at about $2.50 a month on a 27-month term [3].
- ExpressVPN has an independently audited no-log policy and costs under $3 a month on a 28-month term [3]. It renews at a much higher price [3].
- DuckDuckGo VPN passed an audit [1], but it lacks streaming-optimized servers and costs $9.99 a month [1]. It does not fit your criteria.

## Conflicting evidence

A 2022 Tom's Guide article says Proton's speeds are "very impressive" [4]. Newer data disagrees. Comparitech reports Proton has the highest EU latency and noticeable lag when busy [6]. Gizmodo's 2026 tests show a larger speed loss than NordVPN [3]. I weight the recent tested data more heavily. The Tom's Guide article is dated 2022 and flagged as possibly outdated.

## Before you decide

- Check the renewal price and the date of any NordVPN audit report before buying.
- Use the refund window to test your own streaming services.
- Gizmodo is the main source for both the streaming results and the NordVPN audit count. Corroborate it before relying on it.
