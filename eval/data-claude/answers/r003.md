## Recommendation

Buy the **Redmi 15 5G** (starts at Rs 15,999 [1]). It wins on your top priority, battery. It only narrowly beats the Samsung Galaxy A17 5G in the evidence-weighted ranking (66 vs 63.5 out of 100). Treat the two as close.

## Why

- It has a 7,000mAh battery, which Croma calls one of the biggest in the segment [1]. Most rivals are smaller. The Galaxy A17 5G has a 5000mAh (typical) battery [4].
- It promises 2 years of OS updates and 4 years of security updates [1]. That is decent, but see the trade-offs below.
- It has 50MP dual rear cameras and an 8MP front camera [1]. Croma recommends the 256GB version to hold the photos [1]. This is a spec list, not evidence of good indoor performance.
- It starts at Rs 15,999, which leaves room under the Rs 20,000 budget [1].

## Trade-offs

- Samsung offers longer update promises. The Galaxy M17 5G is listed with 6 OS generations and 6 years of security updates [4]. The Galaxy A07 5G has Android 16 with 6 promised years of updates [5]. The Redmi's 2 OS and 4 security years is shorter than both. If long-term updates matter as much as battery, this is the main weakness.
- None of the sources tests indoor or low-light photos on the Redmi. Its camera suitability for photos of your kids is unverified.

## Alternatives

- **Samsung Galaxy A17 5G.** Croma says it has one of the best software update promises in the segment [1], but gives no number of years. It has a 50MP OIS main camera and a 13MP front camera [4]. OIS (optical image stabilisation) usually helps indoor shots, though that is my inference and not in the sources. Choose it if updates and camera matter more than battery size. I found no price for it in the sources.
- **Samsung Galaxy A07 5G.** It has the 6-year update promise [5]. However, it has a 720p LCD and below-average selfies [5], so it is weak for photos.
- **Oppo K13 5G.** It has a 7,000mAh battery with 80W charging and is priced from Rs 19,999 [1]. Digit gives it a camera score of 2.73 [2], but the scale is unstated, and I found no verified update promise.
- **Avoid:** the Realme P4x, whose camera is called rather weak [1], and the Vivo T5 Lite 5G, which Beebom says is slow and bloated [5].

## Conflicting evidence

Croma says the Oppo K13 starts at Rs 19,999 [1]. Digit lists it at Rs 24,999 [2]. I trust Croma more. It is dated, has a named author, and its reliability rose after verification (70 to 76), while Digit's fell (62 to 54). Digit is also undated and may be quoting a different variant. The K13 may therefore be over budget.

## Before you decide

- Evidence is thin. The sources are retailer and listicle pages plus a Samsung marketing page. None has hands-on battery or low-light tests.
- The Redmi's specs and update promise come from a single source [1]. Confirm them on Xiaomi India's listing.
- Check the Galaxy A17's update years and current price on Samsung's site.
- Compare real indoor photo samples before buying.
