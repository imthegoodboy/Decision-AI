## Recommendation

Redmi 15 5G (from Rs 15,999) is my pick, but check its software-update promise before you buy, because the sources disagree on it. The tool's evidence-weighted ranking put the Oppo K13 5G level with it, at 66/100 each. I still chose the Redmi because it is about Rs 4,000 cheaper and had more evidence coverage (65% against 40%). Nothing I verified separates the two on camera or updates.

## Why

- Battery is the best-verified point. The Redmi 15 5G has a 7,000mAh battery, which Croma calls one of the biggest in the segment [1]. Another source also lists 7,000mAh [5].
- It starts at Rs 15,999 [1][5], well inside your budget. Croma advises the 256GB version for photo storage [1].
- It has 50MP dual rear cameras and an 8MP front camera [5][1]. This is only a spec list. Neither source tested low-light photos of children.
- One source says megapixel counts tell you little for indoor shots, and that processing and stabilisation matter more [5]. That is a single opinion.

## Trade-offs

- The indoor-photo evidence is thin. Nothing I verified shows the Redmi is good in dim rooms.
- Samsung's Galaxy A17 5G has only a 5,000mAh battery [4][5]. Croma calls its update promise one of the best in the segment but gives no duration [1].

## Alternatives

- **Oppo K13 5G:** 7,000mAh battery with 80W fast charging, starting at Rs 19,999 [1][5]. Pick it if fast charging matters and you have the extra budget. Higher-storage versions can cost more than Rs 20,000 [5].
- **Vivo T4x 5G:** 6,500mAh battery from Rs 15,499 [1][5]. It is a cheaper option.
- **Galaxy A07 5G:** Ships Android 16 with a promised 6 years of updates [6]. Its main camera is decent, but it has a 720p LCD screen and a soft selfie camera [6].
- **Avoid:** The Vivo T5 Lite 5G is slow and full of bloatware [6]. The Realme P4x 5G has a weak camera setup [1].

## Conflicting evidence

- **Redmi 15 5G updates:** Croma says 2 years of OS updates and 4 years of security updates [1]. The Rupee Gadget Desk says 4 years of OS updates and 6 years of security updates [5]. The tool marked both claims contradicted. I cannot tell which is right. The Rupee Gadget Desk's reliability score fell to 50 after verification, while Croma's rose to 74. That favours Croma's lower figure, but it does not settle the question. Assume 2+4 until you confirm it with Xiaomi.
- **Galaxy A17 5G camera:** Samsung says the main camera has OIS [4]. The Rupee Gadget Desk says it does not, and that this hurts dim-room shots [5]. I did not resolve this.
- **Galaxy M17 5G updates:** Samsung says 6 OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates [4]. The Rupee Gadget Desk says 2 OS upgrades and 4 years of security patches [5]. This is also unresolved. Samsung is the maker but is marketing its own product.

## Before you decide

- Confirm the Redmi 15 5G's update policy on Xiaomi's official site.
- Check the Galaxy A17 5G's OIS on Samsung's spec sheet.
- Look for hands-on low-light camera tests of any phone you shortlist.
- If updates matter more than battery and camera, consider the Galaxy A07 5G [6]. Its verified update promise is the longest.
