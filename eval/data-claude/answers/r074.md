**Recommendation: Samsung Galaxy M17 5G, with the Galaxy A17 5G as a close alternative.** I could not verify prices for either phone, so check current listings before you buy.

**Why the M17**
- Software updates are your long-term priority. Samsung's page says the M17 gets 6 generations of OS upgrades and 6 years of security updates [4]. That is the clearest update promise in these sources. It comes from the manufacturer's own marketing page, so treat it as a claim and not as independent confirmation.
- For photos, the M17 has a 50MP camera with OIS, plus a 5MP ultra-wide and a 13MP front camera [4]. None of the sources tested indoor or low-light shots on it. Samsung's page says only that it takes good photos, so I can't promise how it handles your kids indoors.
- Battery is adequate but not class-leading. It has a 5000mAh battery with 25W charging [4].

**Alternatives**
- **Galaxy A17 5G:** It has a 50MP OIS camera and a 5000mAh battery [4]. Croma, a retailer, calls its software promise "one of the best" in the segment but gives no number [1]. Samsung's page gives no update years for it either [4]. I could therefore only confirm the long update window for the M17.
- **Redmi 15 5G:** This is the battery pick. It has a 7,000mAh battery, which Croma calls one of the biggest in the segment, and it starts at Rs 15,999 [1]. The catch is updates. It is promised only 2 years of OS updates and 4 years of security updates [1]. Choose it only if battery matters more to you than software longevity.
- **Oppo K13 5G:** It also has 7,000mAh and 80W charging, and Croma lists it from Rs 19,999 [1]. Digit lists it at ₹24,999 and gives its camera a low 2.73 rating [2]. The two prices differ, probably because of different variants, so confirm the price. The camera score makes it a poor fit for indoor photos.
- **Galaxy A07 5G:** Beebom notes Android 16 with 6 years of promised updates and a battery that lasts more than a day [5]. It also has a 720p LCD and soft selfies [5]. It is cheaper but a compromise.

**Where the sources disagree or are thin**
- Samsung says most phones in this segment have 5000mAh batteries [4]. Croma and Bajaj Finance show 6,500–7,000mAh models [1][3], so Samsung's phones sit at the lower end of what is available. Bajaj says 7,000mAh phones give all-day or even two-day use [3]. That is a generic claim, not a test result.
- The Samsung page is promotional, and the Bajaj page is a financing site's overview. I gave more weight to Croma [1] and the spec and review notes in Digit [2] and Beebom [5].
- No source measured real battery life or indoor camera quality for these phones. Beebom's other camera picks have no usable detail in the text [5]. Digit's list has gaps and no dates [2].

**Bottom line:** The M17 is the best fit for your priorities, since it gives you updates, an OIS camera and acceptable battery life. If battery matters more to you than updates, choose the Redmi 15 5G. Before you buy, look at reviews of indoor, low-light photos from the M17 or A17.
