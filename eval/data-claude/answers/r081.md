## Recommendation
Take **PTE Academic UKVI**, but first confirm that your university accepts it. It ranked first in the evidence-weighted analysis (76/100, against 65 for IELTS for UKVI), mainly because it has the best-documented speed. If breadth of recognition matters more to you than speed, choose IELTS for UKVI.

## Why
- PTE Academic UKVI is an approved Secure English Language Test (SELT), the kind of test the Home Office requires for most visas [7][2].
- It is the fastest option for which the sources give any evidence. One guide says results often arrive within 48 hours [1]. Pearson reports that 85.7% of test takers got results within 2 days in 2018 [3].
- The UKVI version has the same content and format as the standard test. You can prepare with ordinary PTE practice material, and only the booking and test centre differ [7].
- IELTS for UKVI is also an approved SELT [7], so either test meets the visa requirement.

## Trade-offs
- Some PTE results take longer than 2 days because they are flagged for extra review or security checks [3]. The 2018 statistic is old and comes from Pearson, so treat it as indicative.
- PTE is computer-only, whereas IELTS offers paper or computer in many locations [6]. Source [1] also describes PTE as fully computer-based.
- I found no source that gives IELTS for UKVI turnaround times, so I cannot say whether PTE is actually faster. Evidence coverage for IELTS is only 50%.
- A normal (non-UKVI) test cannot be used where a SELT is required. You must book the UKVI version at an approved centre, and the result must be dated within two years before you apply [7].

## Alternatives
- **IELTS for UKVI:** choose it if you want maximum recognition or prefer paper. IDP says over 13,500 organisations trust IELTS versus around 3,000 for PTE [6].
- **LanguageCert International ESOL SELT:** another approved SELT [7][2].
- **TOEFL iBT, Duolingo or Cambridge:** these are not SELTs. Many UK universities still accept them for degree-level study, because for degree-level study a university may assess your English itself (B2 is needed) [7]. Use them only if your university explicitly lists them.

## Conflicting evidence
Source [6] (IDP) says IELTS is "preferred" and more widely recognised than PTE. Pearson [3] says PTE Academic UKVI is accepted for all UK visas, and [1] calls both tests widely accepted. These claims are not strictly incompatible, since both tests are approved SELTs. I give more weight to the neutral sources [7] and [2], which list both tests as approved. IDP co-owns IELTS, its figures are global rather than UK-specific, and I found no independent confirmation of them. Pearson's claim is also vendor marketing. So IELTS may be somewhat better recognised, but the evidence for that is thin.

## Before you decide
- Check your target universities' English requirements page for accepted tests and score thresholds. Those requirements are the real test of "widest acceptance", and the sources do not give them.
- Check current PTE and IELTS UKVI turnaround times with the providers. The PTE statistic is from 2018, and the pages I read come from vendors or third parties of mixed reliability, including some undated pages.
