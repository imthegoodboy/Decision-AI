**Recommendation: IELTS for UKVI (Academic), with PTE Academic UKVI as the faster backup.**

**Why IELTS for UKVI**

- **Acceptance.** For a Student visa at degree level you need English at CEFR B2. You can prove it with a Secure English Language Test (SELT) taken at an approved centre [3][8]. IELTS for UKVI is on the approved list [2][8]. IELTS is accepted by more than 13,500 organisations, against about 3,000 for PTE [7]. The IELTS side of that comparison comes from IDP, which co-owns IELTS, so treat it as a promotional source.
- **Same preparation.** The UKVI version has the same content and format as standard IELTS, so ordinary practice material works [8]. It also offers paper or computer delivery and a face-to-face speaking test [7].
- **Book the right version.** A normal IELTS cannot be used for a visa that needs a SELT. You must book IELTS for UKVI at an approved centre [8].

**The speed trade-off**

- PTE Academic UKVI is fully computer-based and AI-scored, and one guide says results often arrive within 48 hours [1]. Pearson's own figure is that 85.7% of PTE Academic candidates got scores within 2 days in 2018 [4].
- One 2026 guide says UKVI-version PTE results take 7–10 working days because of identity cross-checks [3]. I found nothing in the other sources to confirm that. Check current turnaround with the test provider before you rely on the 48-hour figure.
- If speed matters most, PTE Academic UKVI is a legitimate choice. It is approved for student visas [1][8].

**Conflicting claims to check**

- **Duolingo UKVI.** One source says Duolingo English Test UKVI was added as a SELT in 2026 [3]. Another, checked in September 2026, lists no Duolingo SELT. It says Duolingo is not a SELT, though some universities accept it for Student visas [8]. I would not plan around it.
- **Result validity.** One source says an IELTS for UKVI result is valid for only 12 months [3]. Another says any SELT result must be from the two years before you apply [8]. Confirm the validity window on GOV.UK. Do not test too early, in case your intake moves.

**Other options**

- LanguageCert International ESOL SELT is also approved [2][8].
- TOEFL iBT and Cambridge C1 Advanced are not SELTs. Many universities accept them anyway, because a university that is a Higher Education Provider may assess your English itself [8]. Sheffield, for example, accepts all IELTS qualifications for direct entry to postgraduate courses [2].

**Practical steps**

1. Check your offer letter or university page first. It names the test and score you need, and that score is often above the visa minimum [3].
2. Book the UKVI version at an approved centre.
3. Leave time for a resit.

If your university accepts it, PTE Academic UKVI is the better pick when you are short on time.
