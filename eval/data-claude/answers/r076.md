**Recommendation: Sony ULT WEAR**

For a 90-minute daily train commute, I'd start with the Sony ULT WEAR. SoundGuys picked them as its best overall pair under $200, mainly for effective ANC, long battery life and a compact, foldable over-ear design [6]. In lab tests they lasted 32 hours 35 minutes and attenuated more than 25dB above 80Hz, which suits train commutes [6]. They also earned SoundGuys' "Best ANC" and "Best for Comfort" recognition [6]. Those are the two things you said matter most.

Two caveats:
- **Bass tuning:** SoundGuys found them bass-forward and suggests using the app EQ to tame the ULT button's heavy bass boost [6]. You can fix this in software, and you said bass matters less to you.
- **Price:** SoundGuys' limit is in dollars, and the sources give no UK price for the ULT WEAR [6]. Check the current £ price before buying, because I can't confirm it fits your £200 limit.

**Alternatives**
- **Soundcore Space 2 (comfort first):** Anker describes it as lightweight, with a pressure-relieving headband, protein leather cushions and memory foam. It claims up to 50 hours with ANC on and 4-stage low-frequency noise cancelling for engine and road rumble [5]. This is Anker's own marketing and there is no independent lab data here, so treat the claims with some caution.
- **Soundcore Space One (Android users):** SoundGuys' pick for Android. It lasted 42 hours 57 minutes with ANC on, supports LDAC, and has adjustable ANC strength. It folds, feels lightweight and comes with a wired cable [6].
- **Soundcore Space Q45 (value):** Anker lists adaptive ANC, up to 50 hours of ANC playback and multipoint [5].
- **Sony WH-1000XM5 (stretch pick):** What Hi-Fi? found them comfortable, with just the right clamping force. It also rated them among the class leaders at isolating you from train and Underground drones [1]. They launched at £380, though, and What Hi-Fi? says discounts are likely if you shop around [1]. Whether they fall below £200 is something to check yourself. The same review says Bose's QuietComfort Ultra cut out even more noise [1].

**Skip for your needs:** The JBL Live 670NC has strong measured ANC but is an on-ear design [6]. Which? notes that on-ear headphones are not all comfortable for long listening sessions [7], so I'd avoid it for a daily 90-minute ride.

**Practical tip:** Passive isolation from the ear-cup seal helps with voices, while ANC handles low rumble [5]. Try the headphones on, or buy from a retailer with easy returns, to check the fit over your commute.
