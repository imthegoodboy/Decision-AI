**Why the Sony ULT WEAR**

SoundGuys picked the ULT WEAR as its best overall pair under $200. It also credits them as best for ANC, best for comfort and best over-ear [6]. In lab testing they gave more than 25dB of attenuation above 80Hz, which suits commuting, and the battery lasted 32 hours 35 minutes [6]. That covers a week of 90-minute rides each way. ANC works best on steady, low-frequency noise such as train rumble, and the ear-cup seal handles voices and chatter [5]. The ULT WEAR also have multipoint Bluetooth, a wired 3.5mm option and a foldable body [6].

One caveat for you: they are bass-forward, and SoundGuys suggests using the app EQ to tame the ULT button's heavy bass boost [6]. Since bass isn't your priority, turn it down in the app.

**Alternatives**

- **Soundcore Space 2:** Anker's own guide recommends it for all-day comfort. It has a pressure-relieving headband, soft protein-leather cushions and memory foam. It also claims up to 50 hours of battery with ANC on [5]. That guide comes from the manufacturer, and the sources give no independent ANC measurements for it [5]. It's a good choice if comfort matters most and you want to spend less.
- **Sennheiser Accentum Wireless ($189.95):** Major HiFi describes its ANC as effective for everyday environments, with long battery life. It is also bass-forward [4].
- **Sony WH-1000XM5:** What Hi-Fi? says comfort is "up there with the best" and the clamping force is just right. It calls the ANC among the class leaders for train and Tube drones [1]. However, it launched at £380, and the Bose QuietComfort Ultra, which What Hi-Fi? says cancels even more noise, launched at £450 [1]. Both are over your budget unless you find a big discount [1].

**What I'd avoid**

The JBL Live 670NC has strong measured ANC, but it is an on-ear design [6]. On-ear headphones are not always comfortable for long listening sessions [7], so it fits your 90-minute commute less well. The JLab JBuds Lux ANC is a good budget pick, but its ANC doesn't match higher-end models [6].

**Recommendation**

Buy the Sony ULT WEAR if the UK price is under £200. Pick the Soundcore Space 2 if you want a cheaper, comfort-first pair. If you want the best ANC and comfort and can stretch the budget, watch for an XM5 discount [1].
