## Recommendation
Open **Marcus by Goldman Sachs** for your $15,000 emergency fund. It had the top evidence-weighted ranking (73/100), mainly because it is the simplest no-fee, no-conditions option.

## Why
- CBS News says Marcus has no monthly fee, no minimum opening deposit, no minimum balance to earn the full APY, and no cap on withdrawals or transfers [2]. A second source also says Marcus has no fees or minimum deposit [4].
- The 3.50% APY (CBS, May 2026) needs nothing beyond opening and funding the account, so you don't have to meet deposit conditions [2].
- On transfers, one source says Marcus allows same-day transfers up to $100,000 and lets you link external accounts [4]. That page is dated 2023 and no other source repeats the claim, so treat it as likely but unconfirmed.
- Savings accounts of this type are described as federally insured deposit accounts [2].

## Trade-offs
- 3.50% is lower than several rivals, and all rates are variable and can change without notice [2].
- Marcus has no checking account and no ATM access, so you need a separate checking account [2].
- None of the sources explicitly states that Marcus is FDIC insured. Only the generic statement above [2] covers it, so verify this before depositing.

## Alternatives
- **Vio Bank**: 4.00% on all balances with no required monthly activity and no monthly fees [6]. It has no checking account, and you must transfer money to an external bank to spend it [6]. That makes it weaker on easy transfers.
- **EverBank**: 3.90% with no minimum opening deposit, no monthly fees and no required monthly behavior [6]. Its sources cover fewer of your criteria (it scored lower on evidence coverage).
- **Axos Bank**: a higher rate, but you must meet deposit or balance requirements or it drops to a 1.00% base rate [2][6]. Fortune says Axos offers expanded FDIC coverage of $2.5 million [6]. That claim has only one source.
- **Synchrony**: no fees and no minimum balance [4][2].
- **SoFi**: no monthly fees and no minimums, but its 4.00% includes a six-month new-member boost [2].
- **LendingClub**: 4.00% only if you deposit $250 or more each month, otherwise 3.00% [2][6].
- **Ally**: a 2017 review says it has no monthly maintenance fee [5]. That source is old.

## Conflicting evidence
The sources disagree on Axos's top rate. CBS (May 2026) lists 4.21% [2]. Fortune (July 2026) says up to 5.00% [6]. The tool rated CBS as more reliable after verification (89 versus 66), and the tool marked the 5.00% claim contradicted. Both agree the rate falls to a 1.00% base if conditions are missed [2][6]. I can't resolve the exact figure. Check Axos directly if you are considering it.

## Before you decide
- Confirm on Marcus's website and in the FDIC's BankFind tool that the account is FDIC insured. That is your key requirement, and the sources don't state it for Marcus.
- Check the current APY and transfer terms. The sources date from May to July 2026, plus an undated-rate 2023 page.
- If higher yield matters more than having one place for everything, Vio or EverBank may suit you better.
