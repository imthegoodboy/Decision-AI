**Recommendation: IELTS for UKVI (Academic) is the safest choice for widest acceptance. PTE Academic UKVI is the better choice if speed matters most.** You said you want both, so here is how to decide.

**Why IELTS for UKVI Academic for acceptance**

The UK Home Office accepts only Secure English Language Tests (SELTs) taken at approved centres for most visa routes, so you must book the UKVI version of whichever test you choose [1][7]. IELTS for UKVI is described as well established and accepted by a wide range of UK institutions [1]. IDP, which co-owns IELTS and so has a commercial interest, says over 13,500 organisations trust IELTS while only around 3,000 accept PTE. It argues that IELTS is the choice if you want the widest acceptance and want to avoid retaking a different test later [6]. IELTS also offers paper-based or computer-based delivery in many locations, while PTE is computer-only [6]. Treat these figures as vendor claims.

**Why PTE Academic UKVI for speed**

PTE Academic UKVI is fully computer-based and AI-scored, and results often arrive within 48 hours [1]. Pearson reported that 85.7% of PTE Academic test takers received scores within 2 days in 2018 [3]. That is older data, and some results take longer because of extra review or security checks [3]. I found nothing in these sources that gives IELTS turnaround times, so I can't make a direct speed comparison.

**Important points for a master's applicant**

- Both IELTS for UKVI Academic and PTE Academic UKVI are accepted for study in the UK, and both assess all four skills [6]. UKVI-approved PTE is described as suitable for student visas [1].
- Degree-level study needs CEFR B2. You can prove it with a SELT, but some universities that are Higher Education Providers assess English themselves [7].
- Those universities can therefore accept non-SELT tests such as TOEFL iBT, Duolingo or Cambridge C1 Advanced [7]. Sheffield, for example, accepts all IELTS qualifications for postgraduate entry [2]. These tests are not SELTs, so they cannot be used where the Home Office asks for a SELT [7].
- The content and format of UKVI tests match the standard tests. The difference is the booking and the approved centre, so standard practice material works [7].
- A SELT result must be from the two years before you apply [7].
- Other approved SELTs include LanguageCert and PSI Skills for English UKVI (outside the UK) [7].

**Practical advice**

1. Check your university's English requirements page and your offer letter first. They tell you which tests and scores are needed, and whether a non-SELT test is acceptable [7].
2. If your university accepts both and you need the result quickly, take PTE Academic UKVI and book a slot early to allow for a resit.
3. If you want one certificate that almost any institution or employer will recognise, or you prefer pen and paper, take IELTS for UKVI Academic [6].
4. Always choose the UKVI version at an approved centre, because a standard IELTS result cannot be used for a visa that requires a SELT [7].
5. Confirm the required CEFR level and matching score for your route on GOV.UK before you book [7].
