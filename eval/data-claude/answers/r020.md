**Recommendation: the IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate on Coursera**

It is the best fit for your three requirements: Python, a recognised certificate and hands-on work. Coursera describes it as Python-focused. It is built for beginners and covers Python, Jupyter notebooks, Python libraries, SQL and Excel [1]. It carries IBM's name, and Dataquest says platform certificates from Google, IBM and Harvard have strong brand recognition. It adds that they are completion-based rather than proctored exams [4]. A reviewer who is studying it describes hands-on labs and peer-reviewed assignments [7]. That is one person's account, and the sources don't say whether there is a capstone project.

**Time:** Coursera estimates 4 months at 10 hours a week [1]. At your 5 hours a week, that is roughly 8 months. This is my own extrapolation and not a figure from the sources. The reviewer was also slow while working full time, at about a month per course [7]. Plan for the long haul. Dataquest warns that a shorter programme you finish beats a bigger one you abandon [4].

**Where the sources disagree**
- **Course count:** Coursera says IBM has 11 courses [1], but the reviewer says 9 [7]. Coursera's own page is more likely to be current, since the reviewer wrote in 2024. Check the live course page.
- **Accreditation:** One blog calls Coursera and edX certificates "accredited" [2]. Dataquest says Harvard's CS50P is "not accredited academic offerings" and is a completion credential [4]. I would trust Dataquest here, so treat any of these as a recognised brand name, not a formal qualification.
- **Conflicts of interest:** The Coursera article is Coursera's own marketing [1]. Dataquest sells a competing product [4]. The Coursera-versus-edX piece is a general overview with little detail [6]. Treat all three claims with some caution.

**Cost:** Coursera lists Professional Certificates from $49 a month and Coursera Plus at $399 a year [2]. Given the long timeline, the monthly cost adds up, so a faster pace saves money.

**Alternatives**
- **Meta Data Analyst Certificate (Coursera):** It has five courses and covers pandas, matplotlib and statistics, with a marketing and business focus. Coursera estimates 5 months at 10 hours a week [1].
- **Google Data Analytics Certificate:** It teaches R, not Python, so it doesn't suit your goal [1].
- **edX:** It offers academic depth and the MicroMasters credit route [6]. Harvard's CS50P gives a free certificate, or a $219 verified one. It runs about 100–150 hours and has a final project [4], but it is general Python. The topics listed there don't include data analysis libraries [4].
- **DataCamp:** One review says it is more hands-on and can be cheaper. The same review says it isn't accredited and offers no one-to-one mentorship [2]. I found no other sources on DataCamp, so my evidence on it is thin.
- **Dataquest:** It teaches through data projects at 5–10 hours a week, and its own page says it is portfolio-based [4]. It doesn't give a certificate with the same brand weight.

**Bottom line:** Choose IBM on Coursera if the certificate matters. Start with a free preview or the first module [1]. If you care most about projects, add a portfolio project of your own, because Dataquest says hiring managers value what you can build above credentials [4].
