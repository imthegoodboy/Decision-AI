**Recommendation: Bitwarden Families**

Bitwarden fits your three priorities best: security record, independent audits and price. A family of four fits within its 6-user Families plan. Bitwarden has apps for iOS and Android, and desktop apps for Windows, macOS and Linux [2]. Treat this as a well-supported pick, not a certain one, because the sources disagree on some details.

**Audits and track record.** Several sources say Bitwarden's open-source code has been independently audited more than once [5]. One says it publishes Cure53 audit results [6]. Another lists SOC 2 Type 2 and published third-party audits [8]. Source [2] claims a January 2026 Cure53 audit with zero critical vulnerabilities. I would not rely on that claim alone. The page carries other unverified-sounding performance claims, and no other source confirms that audit. I found no breach of Bitwarden in the sources, but that is absence of evidence, not proof.

**Price disagreement.** Two sources give Families as $40/year for 6 users [2][5]. A third, which says it checked vendor pricing pages in September 2026, gives $47.88/year [8]. I trust the September 2026 figure more. Either way it is the cheapest credible audited option, and prices change often, so confirm on Bitwarden's site. Bitwarden also has a free tier with unlimited devices [2][8].

**Alternatives**
- **1Password Families:** it publishes Cure53 audits and holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 [8]. It covers 5 users and costs $5.99/month, about $72/year, with no free tier [8]. It is the better choice if you value polish over price.
- **RoboForm Family:** it is the cheapest, at $1.59/month promotional or $47.75/year list [1][4][8]. It has a SOC 2 Type 2 report [8] and a third-party audit [1]. Two sources say it has had no breaches in 25 years [1][4]. Both look like affiliate-style reviews, so that claim is weaker evidence. RoboForm's family plan is for five users [1], so four still fits.
- **NordPass:** it has a published third-party audit [8] and a 6-user family plan [1][4]. Its low prices are promotional, and renewal costs more [1]. Reported prices vary widely: $2.79 to $3.69 per month in [1], and $6.24 per month list in [4].

**Avoid LastPass.** Its 2022 breach exposed encrypted vaults and unencrypted metadata [6][8]. The UK ICO fined it £1.2 million [8]. A June 2026 incident also exposed customer contact data, though not vaults [3]. Source [3] comes from competitor Keeper, so its claims about Keeper being "most certified" and "never breached" are marketing. The LastPass facts are corroborated elsewhere [6][8].

**Caveats.** Most sources are comparison sites, and some may earn affiliate income [4]. I did not see the audit reports themselves. Before you commit, check Bitwarden's own audit page and pricing.
