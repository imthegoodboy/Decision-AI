**Recommendation: NordVPN on its two-year Basic plan, with Proton VPN as the close runner-up.**

**Why NordVPN**
- **Streaming:** In Gizmodo's tests NordVPN unblocked all 14 services tried. Pluto TV and Prime Video detected it now and then, and both worked on the next attempt [3].
- **Speed:** Gizmodo recorded an average speed loss of 7.8% and its fastest US result, 181.50 Mbps on a 190.36 Mbps line [3]. Comparitech's Q3 2026 benchmark ranks NordVPN among the three fastest providers, with a speed score of 8.3 out of 10. Proton scored 6.8 [6].
- **Audits:** Gizmodo says six independent audits since 2018 back NordVPN's no-logs policy [3]. None of the other sources describes those audits, so I could not check this claim independently.
- **Price:** The Basic plan is under $3.50 a month on a two-year term [3]. Gizmodo's cost guide gives about $3.50 [7], and Tom's Guide gives $3.49 on a 27-month plan [4]. All of these are under your $5 limit.

**Strongest audit evidence: Proton VPN**
Proton's audit is the best documented. Securitum completed its fifth consecutive annual audit in May 2026. It found no retained browsing activity, DNS queries or connection metadata. The audit covered both free and paid servers [2][5]. Securitum itself notes that this is a point-in-time review of sampled servers, not a continuous guarantee [2].

Proton costs under $3 a month on a two-year plan [3]. Tom's Guide lists $2.99 [4], and Gizmodo's cost guide lists $2.50 [7].

**Where the sources disagree**
Proton's streaming results conflict. Gizmodo's October 2026 tests found it unblocked 10 of 14 services, failing Hulu, Pluto TV and ITVX, and average speed loss was 10.2% [3]. Tom's Guide says it unblocked every site it tried [4]. Tom's Guide's page is dated 2022 and its streaming testing may be older, so I trust Gizmodo's recent, service-by-service table more. Comparitech's benchmark also shows Proton with the highest EU latency and noticeable lag under load [6]. If you rely on Hulu or ITVX, choose NordVPN.

**Other alternatives**
- **ExpressVPN:** The Basic plan is under $3 a month on a 28-month term. It has an independently audited policy and also unblocked all 14 services. It renews at a much higher price [3].
- **Surfshark:** It costs about $2.50 a month on a 27-month term. It unblocked 13 of 14 services and was audited by Deloitte in 2023 and 2025 [3].
- **IPVanish:** Schellman completed its third no-logs audit in September 2026 [8]. My sources contain no streaming results for it, and Comparitech scores its speed lower [6].
- **Not recommended:**
  - PrivadoVPN is very cheap but has not been independently audited [4].
  - DuckDuckGo VPN's audit is genuine, but it costs $9.99 a month and has no streaming-optimised servers [1].

**Caveats**
- The advertised prices apply to the first term only. Renewals are usually two to three times higher, and NordVPN's increases the most [7].
- Both Gizmodo pages carry "Visit" links to the providers, so check the current price at checkout [3][7].
- Streaming unblocking changes often.
