## Recommendation

Sony ULT WEAR is the best-evidenced over-ear under budget in the sources I had. My confidence is moderate, because most of the evidence comes from one review site.

The tool's evidence-weighted ranking put the Sony WH-1000XM5 first (79.5 against 71.5). I still recommend the ULT WEAR because the XM5 launched at £380, which is well over your £200 limit [1]. The XM5 only wins if you can find it discounted to £200 or less.

## Why

- SoundGuys named the ULT WEAR its best overall pick under $200. It praised the effective ANC in a compact, foldable over-ear design [6].
- Lab testing measured more than 25dB of attenuation above 80Hz, which SoundGuys calls well suited to commutes [6].
- The ULT WEAR also earned SoundGuys' "Best for Comfort" recognition [6]. That is a single line with no detail, so treat it as encouraging rather than proven.
- Train rumble is steady, low-frequency noise, which ANC handles best [5]. Weight and soft cushions can matter as much as ANC strength on long train journeys [5].

## Trade-offs

- The ULT WEAR is bass-forward. SoundGuys suggests using the app EQ to tame the ULT button's heavy bass boost [6]. You said bass matters less to you, so this is a minor issue.
- I found no GBP price for it in the sources. The $200 figure is a US budget, not a UK price.
- The Sony XM5 and the Bose QuietComfort Ultra are stronger on noise cancellation. What Hi-Fi? says the Bose cuts out even more noise than the XM5 [1]. Bose is rated above the Sony, but it is far over budget (£450) [1].

## Alternatives

- **Sony WH-1000XM5:** What Hi-Fi? calls its comfort "up there with the best" and says it is among the class leaders at isolating train noise [1]. It launched at £380 [1]. The review says you are likely to find a discount, but gives no figure [1]. Check the current price.
- **Anker Soundcore Space One:** SoundGuys says it folds and feels lightweight [6]. Its ANC strength is adjustable, but SoundGuys gives no ANC measurement [6].
- **Sennheiser Accentum ($189.95):** One brief source calls its ANC effective and its tuning bass-forward [4]. It gives no comfort details, so I can't verify it for your priorities.
- **Anker's Space 2 and Q45:** Anker's own blog pitches these as comfortable and adaptive [5]. That is manufacturer marketing, not independent testing.
- **JBL Live 670NC:** It has strong measured ANC, but it is on-ear, not over-ear [6]. It is a poor fit for 90 minutes of daily comfort.

## Conflicting evidence

The sources do not contradict each other on the main picks. The one tension is the XM5's price. Launch price (£380) is over budget, but What Hi-Fi? says discounts are likely [1]. This is unresolved because no discounted figure is given. Ideal Home ranks the older Sony XM3 first [2]. Its date metadata is 2019, which is outdated, so I gave it little weight.

## Before you decide

- Check current UK prices. The under-$200 picks are quoted in USD only, and I have no UK price for the ULT WEAR.
- The ULT WEAR claims come mainly from SoundGuys. Look for a second review to confirm them.
- Several sources are dated 2019 to 2022, so I leaned on the newer ones.
- Try the headphones on if you can, because clamping force and comfort over 90 minutes vary from person to person.
