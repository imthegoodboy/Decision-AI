**Recommendation: Sony ULT WEAR, with a check on UK price first**

SoundGuys' under-$200 roundup names the Sony ULT WEAR its best overall pick. It is a foldable over-ear that SoundGuys says combines effective ANC, long battery life and broad connectivity [6]. In lab tests it gave more than 25dB of attenuation above 80Hz, which SoundGuys calls well suited to commutes [6]. It also earned SoundGuys' "Best ANC" and "Best for Comfort" recognition, the two things you prioritise [6]. Its battery lasted 32 hours 35 minutes in testing [6], which covers a 90-minute daily commute for roughly a fortnight.

There are two caveats. First, SoundGuys says the ULT WEAR is bass-forward and suggests using the app EQ to tame the ULT button's bass boost [6]. That matters less to you, but you may want to adjust it. Second, this is the only source that covers the ULT WEAR, and it prices in dollars. None of the sources gives a UK price, so confirm it is under £200 before buying.

**Alternatives**
- **Sony WH-1000XM5:** What Hi-Fi? rates the comfort "up there with the best" and says the ANC is among the class leaders at isolating train and Underground drone [1]. It launched at £380 [1], well over budget. The review says only that discounts are likely, without giving a figure [1]. Treat it as a option only if you see it on sale for £200 or less. The same review says the Bose QuietComfort Ultra cancels even more noise [1].
- **JLab JBuds Lux ANC:** This is SoundGuys' value pick. It has "respectable" ANC that does not match higher-end models, measured at 10–20dB below 700Hz, plus 44-hour battery life [6]. It suits a tighter budget but is a step down on noise cancellation.
- **Anker Soundcore Space One:** SoundGuys' pick for Android users. It is lightweight and folds, and it lasted nearly 43 hours with ANC on [6].
- **Sennheiser Accentum:** Major HiFi calls its ANC "effective" but its sound bass-forward, at $189.95 [4]. That review gives no measurements, so the evidence is thin.

**Where sources disagree or are weak**
- Major HiFi describes the JBL Live 670NC as comfortable [4]. SoundGuys lists it as on-ear, and I would avoid it for you [6]. Which? notes that not all on-ear headphones are comfortable for long listening [7]. Of the two, SoundGuys is more credible because it tests with a lab measurement rig and also wears each pair day to day [6].
- Anker's guide promotes its own soundcore Space 2 for all-day comfort [5]. That is a manufacturer's marketing claim with no independent testing, so I would not rely on it.
- The Ideal Home guide is dated 2019 and recommends products, such as the Sony WH-1000XM3 at £259.99, that are over budget [2]. The Sony WF-1000XM5 review covers earbuds, not over-ears [3].

Overall confidence is moderate. Comfort evidence is mostly one reviewer's opinion, and no source tests all of these models against each other.
