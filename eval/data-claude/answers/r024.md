## Recommendation
Choose **Coursera**, specifically the IBM Data Analyst or Meta Data Analyst Professional Certificate. Skip Google's certificate, because it teaches R, not Python. Evidence is moderate, not strong.

## Why
- IBM's certificate focuses on Python and SQL for data analysis [1]. Meta's five-course certificate also covers Python [1]. Google's is "R programming focused" [1].
- Coursera offers certificates in collaboration with major universities and companies [2][6]. Platform certificates from Google and IBM carry strong brand recognition, though they are completion-based, not proctored exams [4].
- Auditing a Coursera course for free gives no completion certificate, so you must pay for the certificate itself [2].

## Trade-offs
- Time is the main problem. A 300-hour programme at five hours a week takes over a year [4]. Only about 12% of people finish MOOCs, a figure from a blogger citing unnamed research [8].
- I found no verified evidence on how project-heavy the Coursera certificates are. Hiring managers reportedly value what you can build over credentials [4], so plan your own portfolio project.
- Verified claims about hands-on learning are thin for every option.

## Alternatives
- **DataCamp**: described as more hands-on for data topics [2]. Its credential status is unverified (see below).
- **edX / Harvard CS50P**: Harvard offers a free certificate [4]. It is a general programming course, not data analysis, so you would still need pandas and visualisation elsewhere [7]. edX has no subscription plans [6].
- **Dataquest**: teaches Python through hands-on data analysis projects [4]. That comes from Dataquest's own blog, so treat it cautiously. The tool's evidence-weighted ranking put it narrowly first (65 against 63 for Coursera). I still recommend Coursera because you asked for a recognised certificate and I verified no Dataquest certificate claim.

## Conflicting evidence
- **DataCamp accreditation**: source [7] says DataCamp's certifications are "accredited and recognised for academic credit". Source [2] says DataCamp is not accredited by any institute or university. [7] is the newest source but the only one making the claim, and the tool lowered its reliability after verification. [2] is older (2024). I treat the claim as unverified, so check DataCamp's own site.
- **IBM duration**: Coursera's article says 4 months at 10 hours a week [1]. [7] says roughly 8 months, attributed to Coursera. I trust [1] as the primary source, but even 4 months at 10 hours is about 160 hours (my arithmetic), or roughly eight months at five hours a week. I did not verify the 8-month figure.
- **Coursera Plus price**: [7] says $79 a month or $599 a year. [2] and [6] say $59 and $399, from 2024. Prices may have risen, but I cannot confirm that.

## Before you decide
- Check the current IBM and Meta durations and prices on Coursera.
- Check DataCamp's certificate accreditation directly.
- Review course syllabi for project work.
- Consider a cheaper trial of a hands-on platform alongside Coursera. Source reliability is modest overall, and no source is peer-reviewed.
