**Recommendation: NordVPN on its two-year Basic plan, with a price check before you buy.**

**Why NordVPN**
- **Audits:** NordVPN has six independent audits since 2018 backing its no-logs policy [3]. Gizmodo's pros list also calls it a "repeatedly audited no-log policy" [3].
- **Streaming:** It unblocked all 14 services in Gizmodo's tests. Pluto TV and Prime Video detected it now and then, and both played on the next attempt [3].
- **Speed:** Its US server tested at 181.50 Mbps on a 190.36 Mbps line, with an average speed loss of 7.8% [3]. In Comparitech's Q3 2026 lab tests it was one of the three fastest providers, behind Mullvad and ahead of Proton [6].
- **Price:** Gizmodo puts the Basic plan under $3.50 a month on a two-year term [3] and at about $3.50 in its cost guide [7]. An older Tom's Guide piece lists $3.49 on a 27-month plan [4].

**Where sources disagree**
- **NordVPN's price:** One source, The Bandwidth Ledger, says NordVPN's cheapest plan is now $5.99 a month after a 2026 pricing overhaul, which would put it over your limit [8]. It is the only source saying so. The Gizmodo pieces are the most recent and agree with each other, though both carry affiliate links [3][7]. I weight the $3.50 figure more, but I can't rule out regional or promotional differences. Confirm the checkout price. [7] also says NordVPN has the steepest renewal increase, so check that too.
- **Proton's audit scope:** [8] says the 2026 Securitum audit sampled only free-tier servers. CyberInsider, which reports on the audit report itself, says the auditors examined selected servers from both free and paid offerings [2]. I trust [2] over [8], but the audit was a point-in-time review of sampled servers, not the whole network [2].

**Alternatives**
- **Proton VPN** (about $2.50 to $2.99 a month on two years [7][4]) has a fifth consecutive annual Securitum audit [2][5] and open-source apps [5]. It is weaker for streaming, though. It unblocked 10 of 14 services and missed Hulu, ITVX and Pluto TV [3]. Its speed loss was 10.2% [3]. It is the better pick if privacy matters most.
- **Surfshark** (about $2.50 a month on 27 months) unblocked 13 of 14 services. Deloitte audited it in 2023 and 2025, and it has no device limit [3].
- **ExpressVPN** (under $3 a month on 28 months) also unblocked 14 of 14 services and has an independently audited policy. It renews at a much higher price [3].

**Treat with caution**
- [8] recommends PrivadoVPN, citing a first independent audit in early 2026. Only [8] says this, and the older Tom's Guide piece says it had no audit [4]. I couldn't corroborate the audit, so I wouldn't rely on it.
- Mullvad is fast [6], but it costs about $5.50 a month regardless of plan length [7], which is over your limit.
- DuckDuckGo's VPN passed a Securitum audit, but it costs $9.99 a month and has no streaming-optimized servers [1].

**Uncertainty:** The streaming and speed tests come mainly from Gizmodo, a single outlet with affiliate links. Streaming unblocking also changes over time, so use each provider's refund window to test your own services.
