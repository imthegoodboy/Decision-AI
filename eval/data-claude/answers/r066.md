## Recommendation
Use **NordPass Family** for the four of you. Treat **1Password Families** as a close alternative if audit transparency matters more to you than price. The tool's evidence-weighted ranking put 1Password first (72.5) and NordPass third (68), but the scores are close. 1Password's price score was left blank because sources conflict on it, which lifts its total. I chose NordPass because price is one of your three priorities and it has the clearest price evidence. Its audit evidence is weaker, so this is a judgement call.

## Why
- NordPass uses XChaCha20 with a zero-knowledge design, so it cannot read your vault, and its architecture is described as independently audited [7]. Two other sources also say it was third-party audited [1][9].
- It runs on iPhone, Android and Windows [4], and the family plan has six accounts [7].
- Family pricing is $3.69/month on a one-year term, and less on a two-year term [7]. Another source confirms the $3.69 one-year figure [4].

## Trade-offs
- No source names NordPass's auditor or the audit date, so the audit claim is vaguer than 1Password's or Keeper's. Confidence there is moderate.
- Renewal comes with a price increase [1][7].
- Item sharing is clunkier than 1Password's [4].

## Alternatives
- **1Password**: the strongest audit evidence. It publishes regular independent audits, including Cure53 penetration tests, and holds SOC 2 Type 2 and ISO 27001 [9]. Its Secret Key never leaves your devices [9]. It has no free tier [7].
- **RoboForm**: the cheapest. The family plan covers five users at about $1.59/month promotional. It holds SOC 2 Type 2, has passed a third-party audit, and has no reported breach in roughly 25 years [7]. That last point is absence of evidence rather than proof.
- **Keeper**: heavily certified (SOC 2, ISO 27001, FedRAMP High) with no vault breach [7]. It costs $91.99/year for five users, and its apps are enterprise-oriented [7].
- **LastPass**: avoid. The UK ICO fined it £1.2 million over the 2022 incident [9]. A June 2026 third-party breach exposed customer data but reportedly not vaults [3].

## Conflicting evidence
- **1Password price**: one source says Families is $5.99/month for five people [9]. Another says it renews at $9.99/month [7]. The tool marked both claims contradicted, so treat the real cost as unknown until you check the vendor's page.
- **Bitwarden**: I left it out of the recommendation because the evidence conflicts.
  - Audit result: one source says its January 2026 Cure53 audit found zero critical issues [2]. Another says it found two critical issues [7]. Source [2] is dated 2023 yet describes 2026 events, and its reliability score fell to 54 after checking, while source [7] ended at 67. That favours [7], but neither links the report, so this is unresolved.
  - Seats: two sources say the family plan covers six users [2][9], and one says three [7]. Bitwarden is still open source with multiple independent audits [5].
- Source [3] is a competitor's (Keeper's) blog, so its framing of LastPass is not neutral.

## Before you decide
- Read NordPass's own published audit reports and confirm the renewal price.
- Check current pricing on each vendor's page, since all figures are promotional or vary by source.
- Whichever you pick, have each family member use a long, unique master password [7].
