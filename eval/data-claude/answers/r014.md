**Recommendation: PTE Academic UKVI, provided your university's offer letter or English-requirements page accepts it. If it does not, take IELTS for UKVI (Academic).**

**Why PTE Academic UKVI for speed.** PTE Academic UKVI is a fully computer-based, AI-scored test, and one source says results often arrive within 48 hours [1]. Pearson says 85.7% of test takers got scores within 2 days, and that flagged results can take longer, with contact advised after 5 days [3]. That figure is Pearson's own and comes from 2018, so treat it as indicative, not a guarantee. I found no source giving IELTS for UKVI result times, so I can't compare speed directly.

**Why it is still widely accepted for a UK master's.** PTE Academic is on the UK government's list of Secure English Language Tests (SELTs) [2]. A current guide lists PTE Academic UKVI as an approved SELT [7]. The IELTS-owner article says both tests' UKVI Academic versions are accepted for UK study [6]. For degree-level study the visa requirement is CEFR B2 [7].

**Where the sources disagree.** The IDP article says IELTS is "preferred" and has wider acceptance, with over 13,500 organisations against about 3,000 for PTE [6]. IDP co-owns IELTS and sells it, so it has a commercial interest, and its figures are global, not specific to UK master's visas. Pearson is likewise a PTE vendor, so its speed claim is also self-interested. No source in my set tested acceptance at UK universities directly. I trust the government-aligned SELT lists [2][7] over either vendor, and they put both tests on the approved list. IELTS's "widest acceptance" edge is plausible for employers and other countries, but it is weakly evidenced for your use case.

**Alternatives.**
- **IELTS for UKVI (Academic):** the safest choice if your university names IELTS. You can also choose paper-based, which PTE does not offer [6]. Note that a normal IELTS cannot be used for a visa that needs a SELT. You must book the UKVI version at an approved centre [7].
- **LanguageCert International ESOL SELT:** also an approved SELT [2][7].
- **TOEFL iBT, Duolingo and Cambridge C1:** these are not SELTs, but some universities accept them because they may assess English themselves [7]. Duolingo is not usable where the Home Office requires a SELT [7].

**Practical checks.** Book the UKVI version at an approved centre. The result must be dated within two years before you apply [7]. Confirm the required score with your university and on GOV.UK, because requirements vary [7]. Some universities, such as Sheffield, accept non-SELT tests for entry [2]. I found no India-specific information, such as test-centre availability or booking waits, so check those locally.
