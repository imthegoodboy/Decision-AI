## Recommendation
Shortlist the **TVS iQube**, with low-to-moderate confidence. The evidence-weighted ranking put it first (55/100), followed by Bajaj Chetak, Ather 450 and Ola S1. The margins are small. The sources cover your three priorities (reliability, real-world range, service network) only thinly, so treat this as a test-ride shortlist, not a final verdict.

## Why
- Autocar India's test found the TVS "a step above" the Bajaj Chetak as a comfortable, easy-to-ride city commuter [6].
- TVS and Bajaj kept stable sales and kept growing in 2024, while Ola's sales and market share fell [3]. This is only an indirect hint about service quality.
- Range is promised at 140 km for the iQube's 4.56 kWh battery [4]. A listing says the bigger-battery variants "offer decent range for daily use" [8]. Both are claims, not measured Bengaluru figures. A 20 km commute is well within these numbers, even after allowing for real-world shortfall. One source says Bangalore traffic range runs well below certified figures [2].

## Trade-offs
- No source gives measured real-world range or Bengaluru service data for the iQube.
- The reviews that cover the iQube, Ather and Chetak are from 2022, and the test was about 1,000 km in Mumbai, not Bengaluru [6]. The models have since changed.

## Alternatives
- **Ather 450**: the best-handling scooter tested [6], and its Eco mode stayed usable below 10% charge [6]. It was the least comfortable, and cramped for riders over 5ft 10in [6]. Claimed range is 146 km [4]. I found no verified reliability or service evidence for Ather.
- **Bajaj Chetak**: has an absorbent ride [6] but felt dated in 2022 [4]. Its dealer coverage is not evidenced here.
- **Ola S1**: I would avoid it given the service evidence. In 2024 it received about 80,000 service complaints a month [3], with customers reporting 30–45 day repair waits [3]. It listed 430 service centres [3] and announced a plan for 1,000 by December 2024 [7]. I could not verify that this plan was delivered. The reviewer also noted suspension-failure cases shared online [6].
- **Ampere** (another option): the vendor's own guide claims 420+ service points [2]. This is a self-claim from an undated page, so treat it cautiously.

## Conflicting evidence
No source directly contradicts another. Source [1] is a duplicate of [6], so it is not independent corroboration. The Ola service claims come from several 2024 outlets [3][5][7], but they rest on insiders and anecdotes. The Autocar India review [6] is the most credible source here, according to the verification tool. The vendor page [2] is the weakest.

## Before you decide
- Look for 2026 owner reviews and check the nearest service centres to your home and office in Bengaluru.
- Test ride the iQube, Chetak and Ather. Ather is the one to skip if you are tall.
- Ask current owners for their real range and service turnaround.
- Confirm current prices and variants. One listing gives iQube at ₹95,124–1.30 lakh [8].
