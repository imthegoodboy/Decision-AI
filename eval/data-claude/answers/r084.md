## Recommendation
Choose Coursera, specifically the IBM Data Analyst Professional Certificate, and add one project of your own. This matches the tool's evidence-weighted ranking (Coursera 68.5/100, then Dataquest 55, edX 54, DataCamp 46). Confidence is moderate, because most sources are vendor-published or low-reliability.

## Why
- The IBM certificate focuses on Python and SQL for data analysis [1]. Google's certificate on the same platform is R-based, so avoid it if Python is your goal [1].
- Google and IBM platform certificates carry strong brand recognition, though they are completion-based rather than proctored exams [4].
- Coursera collaborates with hundreds of universities and companies [2][6]. It also offers guided projects for hands-on practice [6], although that point rests on a single low-reliability source.
- Coursera Plus costs around $399 a year [6][2]. This is a US-dollar figure, and the Coursera page itself shows a regional rupee price [1], so check current pricing.

## Trade-offs
- Coursera's time estimate for the IBM certificate is 4 months at 10 hours a week [1]. At your 5 hours a week, I would expect roughly double. That is my own inference, not a stated figure. Another source warns that a 300-hour programme takes over a year at 5 hours a week [4].
- The certificate shows completion, not a proctored test of skill [4].
- One Dataquest article says portfolio projects rank above certificates with hiring managers [4]. Treat that as opinion, since Dataquest sells project-based learning.

## Alternatives
- **Dataquest**: its Python for Data Analysis path teaches through hands-on projects and takes 3-6 months at 5-10 hours a week [4]. Both claims come from Dataquest's own article. The sources show no recognition for its certificate, so it fits best if portfolio matters more than brand.
- **DataCamp**: one article calls its method more hands-on [2]. The same article says it is not accredited by any institute or university [2]. This rests on a single source, and no DataCamp primary source was available.
- **edX**: Harvard's CS50P offers a free certificate, or a $219 verified one [4]. It is an introduction to programming, not data analysis, so it works as a starter only [4].

## Conflicting evidence
- **IBM course count**: Coursera's 2025 page says 11 courses [1]. A 2024 personal blog says 9 [7]. Coursera is more authoritative and more recent, and the difference is minor.
- **edX accreditation**: Upskillwise says edX offers accredited certificates [2]. Dataquest quotes Harvard calling CS50P "not accredited academic offerings" [4]. Analytics Insight says edX has fewer practical industry certificates than Coursera [6]. The tool marked the accreditation claim contradicted, so treat it as unverified.
- Sources [3] and [5] are mirrors of [1] and [2]. They are not independent corroboration.

## Before you decide
- Preview the first module free [1], and check current prices.
- All sources are flagged as possibly outdated.
- A blogger completing the IBM certificate part-time described slow, uneven progress [7], and the same post cites an uncited figure that only around 12% finish MOOCs [7]. Pick a steady weekly schedule.
- The DataCamp and edX evidence is thin.
