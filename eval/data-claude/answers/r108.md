## Recommendation

NordVPN on a two-year plan is my pick. It is the strongest all-round fit for streaming speed at under $5 a month. Proton VPN is a very close second and is the better choice if the audit evidence matters most to you. The tool's evidence-weighted ranking put them at 83 and 82 out of 100, so this is nearly a tie.

## Why

- Gizmodo's tests found NordVPN unblocked all 14 streaming services it tried [3].
- Its US server tested at 181.50 Mbps on a 190.36 Mbps connection, the fastest US result Gizmodo recorded, and average speed loss was 7.8% [3]. Comparitech's separate benchmark also named NordVPN among the fastest providers [6].
- The Basic plan is under $3.50 a month on a two-year term [3]. Two other sources give about $3.50 [7] and $3.49 [4].
- NordVPN's no-log policy is described as backed by six independent audits since 2018 [3]. Only one source states this.

## Trade-offs

- Renewals typically cost two to three times the introductory rate, and Gizmodo says NordVPN increases the most [7]. Long plans are only cheap while the first term lasts.
- Gizmodo noted occasional detection on Pluto TV and Prime Video, though both played on the next attempt [3].

## Alternatives

- **Proton VPN** has the best-documented audits. It has passed a fifth consecutive annual Securitum no-logs audit [5], which a second source also reports [2]. The Plus plan is under $3 a month on two years [3]. It unblocked only 10 of 14 services, including not Hulu or ITVX, with 10.2% average speed loss [3]. Choose it if privacy evidence and price outweigh streaming breadth.
- **ExpressVPN** is described as independently audited, and its Basic plan is under $3 a month on a 28-month term, but it renews at a much higher price [3].
- **Surfshark** had its no-log policy audited by Deloitte in 2023 and 2025 [3]. Gizmodo rated its streaming 13 of 14 [3].
- **DuckDuckGo VPN** has had an independent audit [1], but it lacks streaming-optimized servers [1]. It is not a fit for this need.

## Conflicting evidence

Speed evidence on Proton VPN differs by source. Gizmodo reports a modest 10.2% average speed loss [3]. Comparitech says Proton has the highest latency to EU servers and noticeable lag when busy [6]. These measure different things, so they are not strictly incompatible. Both suggest Proton is not the fastest option, which is one reason I rank NordVPN slightly ahead. Note that Comparitech's page carries a 2019 date but contains 2026-Q3 data [6].

## Before you decide

- NordVPN's "six audits" claim comes from a single source [3]. I did not see the audit reports themselves, so read them on NordVPN's site.
- Proton's audit was a point-in-time review of selected servers, not a continuous guarantee [2].
- Prices change with Black Friday sales. Check the checkout price and the renewal rate, and use the refund window to test your own streaming services.
